# Foreign trade and payments, phase 5, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line: the trade rules that decide U1 are published in three places, the Federal Register, the Chinese ministry notices and the Global Trade Alert register, and two of the four indicators below can be read by a machine today. The Federal Register has a public JSON interface, which I confirmed on 11 October 2026; a term search for the Section 232 medical investigation returns the 26 September 2025 comment request as the newest matching action, with no proclamation (Federal Register API, 11 October 2026). The Chinese truce notice and the producer-country helium measures have no feed; a person reads them. Read date 11 October 2026.

**Id** FT1. **Uncertainty** U1. **Name** China truce renewal notice. **What is measured** Whether the one-year suspension of China's 24 percent additional tariff on United States goods, and the matching United States suspension of the 50 percent affiliates rule, is renewed, lapsed or replaced. **Source name** State Council Tariff Commission notice, carried by Global Times via GlobalSecurity, with wire reports. **Source link** https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/china/2025/11/china-251105-globaltimes04.htm (the page returned a paywall code today; the reading is confirmed by https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/china-says-extends-suspension-of-extra-24-tariff-on-us-goods-1.500334167, 5 November 2025). **Cadence** Event driven; one notice per truce term. **Mode** Manual. **Settles toward A when** A notice renews the suspension for a year or longer before it lapses. **Settles toward B when** The 24 percent duty returns or an entity listing names the China plant (N17) or its logic supplier (N10). **False signal** A renewal reported by wire but not yet on the ministry site, or a short extension of weeks, which keeps the parts flow at N17 and N31 in a queue rather than settling anything. **Last read** The suspension took effect at 13:01 on 10 November 2025 for one year; no renewal notice found on 11 October 2026 (Gulf News, 5 November 2025). The 10 January 2027 date carried by Geopolitical remains unreconciled. **Leans toward** Neither; the term ends in about a month. Nodes N17, N31, N29.

**Id** FT2. **Uncertainty** U1. **Name** Section 232 medical equipment action. **What is measured** Any Commerce report or presidential proclamation under the Section 232 investigation into medical equipment including devices, opened 26 September 2025. **Source name** Federal Register, documents interface. **Source link** https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[term]=%22section+232%22+%22medical+equipment%22&order=newest&per_page=5 **Cadence** Daily, each publication day. **Mode** Live. **Settles toward A when** A proclamation confines duties to finished scanners or exempts imaging equipment, or the statutory clock passes with no action. **Settles toward B when** A proclamation reaches MR components or systems built at the China plant for Asia, which would also change the duty on N17 and N18 flows. **False signal** A Foreign-Trade Zone or unrelated notice that matches the search terms; the reader checks the agency and document type. **Last read** Newest matching document is the 26 September 2025 request for comments; no report or proclamation (Federal Register API, 11 October 2026; Sandler, Travis and Rosenberg status page shows the 17 October 2025 comment deadline as its last entry). **Leans toward** A. Nodes N17, N18, N31.

**Id** FT3. **Uncertainty** U1. **Name** Listing of wire, niobium or cold heads. **What is measured** Any export-control or trade-remedy notice by the United States, China, Japan, the European Union or the United Kingdom that names superconducting wire, niobium, Gifford-McMahon cryocoolers or cold heads. **Source name** Federal Register documents interface for the United States; Global Trade Alert for the other producer countries. **Source link** https://www.federalregister.gov/api/v1/documents.json?conditions[term]=%22superconducting%22+%22export%22&order=newest&per_page=5 and https://globaltradealert.org **Cadence** Daily for the Federal Register; Global Trade Alert posts within days of a measure. **Mode** Live for the United States search; manual for the register. **Settles toward A when** The 2027 sourcing cycle closes with no listing. **Settles toward B when** Any notice names the part; the stop reaches N03, N05 or N17 within a quarter. **False signal** Control entry 1C005 already names niobium-titanium with a non-copper matrix; a notice that re-issues that entry does not reach magnet-grade wire in a copper matrix (Trade Hub, current). **Last read** Newest Federal Register match is the January 2025 artificial intelligence diffusion rule; none of the 44 matches names superconducting wire or cryocoolers (Federal Register API, 11 October 2026). **Leans toward** A. Nodes N02, N03, N05, N17.

**Id** FT4. **Uncertainty** U2. **Name** Producer-country helium export measures. **What is measured** The count and end dates of helium export licensing or bans in force at Russia, China and Qatar, plus any United States allocation or export rule. **Source name** Global Trade Alert interventions 154600 and 157329. **Source link** https://globaltradealert.org/intervention/154600-russia-helium-added-to-the-export-licensing-list and https://globaltradealert.org/intervention/157329-china-temporary-export-ban-on-helium **Cadence** Updated as measures change; checked quarterly. **Mode** Manual. **Settles toward A when** The Russian licensing is extended past 31 December 2027, the Chinese ban stays, or a United States rule appears. **Settles toward B when** Both measures lapse and Qatari volume returns through Hormuz. **False signal** The Russian decree lapsing on its own date while Hormuz stays shut; helium that is legal but not shipped still decides whether a magnet ships. **Last read** Russia Decree 404 in force 14 April 2026, revocation date 31 December 2027; China Announcement 2026/29 in force 10 July 2026, no revocation date (Global Trade Alert, read 11 October 2026). **Leans toward** A. Nodes N01, N19, N34.

## Critical factors

The Federal Register interface makes FT2 and the United States half of FT3 readable by the command centre today. FT1 and FT4 rest on notices with no feed, so a person reads them each quarter and on each truce date. FT1 is the only one that moves within a month. Panel judgement.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the Russian measure's own end date and the truce term. Uncertain, outcomes: whether either is renewed. Uncertain, actions: whether a producer country lists wire or cold heads at all.

## Contradictions with other lenses

Round 1; none read. The truce date split with Geopolitical (10 November 2026 against 10 January 2027) stands; FT1 settles it.

## Blind spots of this lens

A machine search on terms misses a listing that uses a tariff code instead of a word, and misses informal holds at a producer that never become a notice.
