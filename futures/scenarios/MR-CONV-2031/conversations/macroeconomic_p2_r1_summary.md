# Macroeconomic, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

Qatar supplied about a third of world helium in 2025, its Ras Laffan complex was struck on 2 and 18 March 2026, the energy minister put repairs at three to five years, and the Russian alternative remains under a United States and European Union import ban, so the helium origins on the map are narrowed for the whole decision cycle (N01, N19, N23, N34; USGS Jan 2026, Reuters 4 Mar 2026 via Wikipedia, Interfax 30 Jul 2026).

A 50% United States tariff on the copper content of wire, bus bars and other worked copper has applied to every origin since 1 Aug 2025 and a 25% domestic sales rule starts in 2027, while the proposed 15% duty on refined copper from 1 Jan 2027 is still undecided (N04; White House 30 Jul 2025, ING Jun 2026).

Memory makers roughly tripled DRAM contract prices across 2026 and are allocating capacity to AI and server buyers under agreements reported to run into 2028, so the OEM's consoles buy on allocation at least until then (N11, N12; TrendForce 2 Feb, 8 Jul and 30 Sep 2026; Panel judgement on the duration).

The Federal Reserve raised rates to 3.75% to 4.00% in September 2026 and projects 4.1% through 2027, producer prices are up 5.4% a year, and United States hospital margins are 2.5% year to date with bad debt up 17%, so the customer's cost of borrowing and reimbursement are both tighter through the 2027 capital plan (N35, N36; Federal Reserve 16 Sep 2026, BLS 10 Sep 2026, Kaufman Hall via Advisory Board 27 Aug 2026).

The 25% Section 232 chip tariff of 15 Jan 2026 exempts non-data-centre industrial uses, which should cover reconstruction processors, but this is unconfirmed inside the OEM (N10, N12, N08; EY 15 Jan 2026; AI-suggested, Sourcing to confirm).

## In one hundred words

Four things are set by 2031. Qatari helium capacity is impaired on a repair schedule the minister put at three to five years, and Russian helium stays sanctioned, so the US magnet plant's helium origins are narrowed past the 2027 sourcing cycle. Worked copper carries a 50% US tariff already in law. Memory is allocated to AI buyers under contracts into 2028. US hospitals borrow at about 4% with thinner margins. Left for phase 3: the strait's reopening, the refined copper duty, the price of a helium fill, currency, and whether margin pressure cuts or only delays orders.
