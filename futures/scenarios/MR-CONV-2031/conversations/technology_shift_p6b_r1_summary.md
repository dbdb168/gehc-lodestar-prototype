The second cold-head source (A1) works in all four futures, not two, because the same cold head cools conventional and sealed magnets, so it ranks first with a start-by of the second quarter of 2027. N05, N16, N17; Panel judgement.

## In one hundred words

Through the technology lens the cold head is the part every future shares, so qualifying a second maker is the one action that holds in all four futures and carries the highest cost of waiting, given eighteen months of requalification against a 2029 sealed majority. Confirming the Placeholders comes next, because the sealed test draw decides whether helium cover or the cold head is the margin. The manual fallback is third. Conversion pace cannot be set until the test draw is known and a 3 tesla sealed design has service history, where this lens expects disagreement.
