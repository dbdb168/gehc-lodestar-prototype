## Critical factors

Whether a notice names the parts (U1) and whether Hormuz traffic climbs past the mid-2026 level (U2). Confidence that all four read toward A today is high on the registers and moderate on Hormuz, where two sources differ by a factor of ten.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the registers exist and are dated. Uncertain, kind outcomes: what they say by the 2027 sourcing cycle. Kind actions: whether the Section 232 investigation ends in anything.

## Contradictions with other lenses
