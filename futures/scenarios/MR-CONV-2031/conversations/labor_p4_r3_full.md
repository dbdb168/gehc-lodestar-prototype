In F1 and F3, where the US plant carries the Western Hemisphere alone, a port stop in late 2030 holds every export magnet for weeks and lands on an order book already late. Last time, the 2024 strike ran three days before a wage deal. AI-suggested on the OEM's port use; Logistics confirms.
