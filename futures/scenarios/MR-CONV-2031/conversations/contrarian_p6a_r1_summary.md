# Contrarian, phase 6a, round 1, summary

## Key points

The China plant breaches tolerance in all four futures, by allocation queue in F1, by listing in F3 and by the cold head in F2 and F4, so the Eastern Hemisphere break is a shared break the panel has not named as one (N17, N10, N05; Panel judgement).

The 35-day encryption event breaches the line in every future wherever it lands, and it is indistinguishable from the first break when it lands on the cold-head maker in F2 and F4 and worst at the carriers and the port in F1 (N16, N03, N05, N23, N26; Panel judgement, fallback Placeholder unset).

Three risks only cost on every page, the US hub, the wire and the technicians, because no future reaches them, not because the world spares them; the technicians carry the same undated break in all four (N18, N03, N20; Panel judgement).

The dates on the sealed-side breaks are votes, not evidence: F2 says no schedule and then 2029 to 2031, and F4's 2029 rests on an allocation nobody has announced, settled by the parent's segment disclosure (N05; AI-suggested, Sourcing confirms).

Each page names the Placeholder that removes its first break: cover above 60 days at N19 for F1, the maker's address for F2's hazard path, logic stock longer than the licence queue at N17 for F3, and a multi-year agreement with the cold-head parent for F4 (N19, N05, N17; Placeholder).

## In one hundred words

The four futures differ in cause but share their shape. The China plant breaches in every one, the encryption event breaches in every one, and the sealed side always lands on the sole cold head. The hub, the wire and the technicians never break, which means the futures never tested them. The sealed-side dates are votes on an undated event. Confidence is high that the encryption event breaches, moderate on which node each future breaks, low on every date after 2028. Four Placeholders, one per page, each remove a first break if they read the right way.
