# Security, phase 3, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line first. Two uncertainties from this lens move six of the eight risks each, and both turn on the same fact that no public source can show: whether the plants, the two sole suppliers and the design record can run for 30 days without the systems that hold them. Everything else this lens sees moves two or three risks. Phase 2 fixed the rules, the recovery record and the size of the counterfeit and theft markets; what is open is who gets hit, how deep the single point of data runs, and whether a trusted supplier is quietly failing in a way no advisory flags. The research budget for this run is spent, so every figure below comes from the phase 2 transcripts and their sources; no new source has been fetched.

**U-S2. If one of the OEM's three sites, or the one wire mill or the one cold-head maker, is encrypted, does the line stop for more than 30 days?** Moves R1 (N16), R2 (N17), R3 (N18), R4 (N03), R5 (N05) and R8 (N19), because the helium liquefier at the magnet plant runs on the same site controls (that dependence is AI-suggested; Site operations confirms). Kind: outcomes, with a causation element (is there a tested manual fallback for winding, test and shipping records). It could go three ways: no event, or one recovered inside 30 days; an event at a sole supplier with a five-week-plus restart that drains the wire or cold-head buffer before the mill is back; an event at an OEM site that stops a hemisphere's magnets outright. It is uncertain rather than predetermined because the recovery record is real but drawn from a vehicle maker (restart in phases over five weeks, £196 million in the quarter, a £1.5 billion state guarantee; BleepingComputer, 15 November 2025; UK government, 28 September 2025) and a brewer (30 plants disrupted; Food Ingredients First, 18 March 2026), not a magnet maker; the inference to this line is Panel judgement. The resolving observation is a supplier's or the OEM's own disclosure: listed parents file within four business days of a materiality finding (SEC, 26 July 2023), European Union electronics makers report within 24 hours from 11 September 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847), Japanese designated operators from 1 October 2026 (Recorded Future, read 11 October 2026). Inside the OEM, the date and scope of the last tabletop exercise at N16, N17 and N18 that ran the line without its systems; that is AI-suggested until Site operations shows it.

**U-S3. Does the single record of magnet design and qualification stay whole, available and trusted to 2031?** Moves R1, R2, R3 (a plant rebuilt or relocated is rebuilt from the record), R4, R5 and R6 (qualifying a second mill, cold-head maker or logic part starts from the qualification record at N22). Kind: causation, because the question is how many systems the record lives in and who can change it, and that is internal. Directions: intact and backed up offline, in which case every replacement time on the map holds; exfiltrated in a double-extortion event, which does not stop the line but puts the design in the counterfeit and competitor market; corrupted or quietly altered by an insider or a trusted supplier so that requalification stalls while the record is reconstructed. It is uncertain rather than predetermined because the node map itself calls N22 "owned" and "not a tolerance breach on its own" while also saying it is what makes every other replacement slow; both cannot be true if the record is one system. The 524B postmarket plan and bill of materials (21 U.S.C. 360n-2; FDA guidance, February 2026) tell the regulator what the record covers but not where it sits. The resolving observation is internal: an Engineering and Quality and regulatory statement of how many systems hold the record, whether an offline copy exists, and when it was last restored from. AI-suggested until then.

**U-S4. Does a trusted sole supplier quietly cut corners for years, so that wire or cold heads pass inspection and fail at test?** Moves R4 (N03), R5 (N05) and R8 (N19), because a magnet that fails on ramp consumes a helium fill and a test slot (helium use per fill is a Placeholder). Kind: causation. Directions: the mills hold to specification; a mill under parent pressure to serve fusion and physics order books (P18: Bruker 10-K FY2025; Bluefors, 28 March 2023) lets process control drift on its smallest customer; a cold-head maker under the same pressure substitutes a bearing or seal without notice. It is uncertain because no advisory flags it; the only public signal would be a recall or a quality notice, which lags the drift by years. The resolving observation is the OEM's own incoming inspection and ramp-failure rate by lot, which is internal, and the parent companies' segment disclosures on superconductor backlog, which are public in their annual filings. Panel judgement throughout; this is the lens's own blind spot made explicit.

**U-S5. Does state pre-positioning in United States utilities get used against the grid and water feeding the two United States sites?** Moves R1 (N16), R3 (N18) and R8 (N19 through liquefaction power). Kind: causation and outcomes. Directions: never used, and the exposure stays the ordinary one the infrastructure lens already priced (about ten days after Helene; Duke Energy, 30 September 2024); used in a crisis over Taiwan in a way that lengthens an outage past the grid's own recovery; used against water, which stops cryogenic operations without touching the plant's network. It is uncertain because intent is unobservable and the only public evidence is agency advisories, which this run did not fetch; the claim rests on the phase 2 security transcript's uncertainty list and is Panel judgement, low confidence. The resolving observation is the reliability body's assessments (NERC, 29 January 2026, already rates the Carolinas elevated from 2027 and the Midcontinent high from 2028) and any national cyber agency advisory naming utilities in those two states.

**U-S6. Does strategic cargo theft move from truckload electronics to the inbound boards, coils and cold heads that feed the hub, and to the brokers and banks that clear them?** Moves R3 (N18 inbound on N30 and N24), R5 (N05 ships by air from Japan; AI-suggested, Logistics confirms) and R6 (N10 boards in transit). Kind: outcomes. Directions: theft stays with retail electronics and the OEM's lanes see only loss insurance; impersonation of carriers and brokers reaches high-value industrial air freight, so a board set or a cold-head lot disappears between airport and hub; payment-rail impersonation on N31 holds parts at customs for weeks. It is uncertain because the trend is high and rising (2,646 confirmed incidents in 2025, up 18 percent, losses near $725 million; Verisk CargoNet via SDCE, 2026; strategic theft about 30 percent of incidents; Risk and Insurance, 1 July 2026) but concentrated on consumer electronics (Overhaul 2025 report). The resolving observation is the annual CargoNet and Overhaul product-type tables and the OEM's own claims record. Panel judgement on the lane inference.

**U-S7. Does counterfeit programmable logic reach the control boards through the authorised channel while memory and logic stay on allocation?** Moves R6 (N10) and R3 (N18 stops when a board lot is quarantined). Kind: causation. Directions: authorised distribution holds and counterfeit stays in the broker market; allocation into 2028 (P16: TrendForce, 30 September 2026) pushes a board maker to a broker and a lot fails; a cleared part is cloned well enough to pass incoming test and fails in the field, which is a recall, not a line stop. It is uncertain because the counterfeit market is measured (programmable logic above 15 percent of 748 reports in 2025; ERAI 2025 annual report) but the route into this product is not. The resolving observation is the ERAI annual report, customs seizure statistics by part class, and the OEM's board makers' sourcing declarations, which Sourcing holds.

**U-S8. Does China use its amended cyber and data law against a foreign-owned plant during a trade dispute?** Moves R2 (N17) and R1 (N16, which would have to carry the Eastern Hemisphere, which it cannot do under the tender rules in P9). Kind: value and outcomes. Directions: the law stays a compliance cost; data-transfer rules stop the design record moving between N17 and N22, so the two plants diverge; an investigation freezes exports from N17 as a bargaining move. It is uncertain because the law took effect 1 January 2026 (Mayer Brown, 15 December 2025) and its use against a foreign medical OEM has no precedent in this run's sources. The resolving observation is a published enforcement action naming a foreign device maker, and the outcome of the Section 232 medical-equipment investigation (U-T1), which would give the trigger.

**Watch items that move none of the eight.** A terminal ransomware event at N26 (precedent two days at Nagoya; BleepingComputer, 5 July 2023) sits inside tolerance unless the Coast Guard plan due 16 July 2027 is on paper only. The cryptography retirement after 2030 (NIST IR 8547 draft, November 2024) and the console operating system clock (Microsoft lifecycle, read 11 October 2026) load N15 and N33, not the eight.

## Critical factors

The two factors that carry this lens are the depth of the single point of data at N22 and the untested recovery time of N16, N17, N18, N03 and N05. Everything else is a lane or a part class with a measured market.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined, from phase 2: the reporting duties and their dates, the five-week recovery record, the size of the counterfeit and theft markets. Uncertain, above: U-S2 (outcomes), U-S3 (causation), U-S4 (causation), U-S5 (causation and outcomes), U-S6 (outcomes), U-S7 (causation), U-S8 (value and outcomes). U-S3 and U-S4 have an internal component that belongs with U-L1 in the baseline.

## Contradictions with other lenses

Round 1 is isolated. The contradiction to expect is with the node map's note that a cyber event at N15 is a security exposure, not a supply one; this lens says the same event with N22 is a supply event. The infrastructure lens prices grid outages at days; this lens says a deliberate one is not bounded by the same record. What settles both is a tabletop at the plant with the systems off.

## Blind spots of this lens

I rank the attacker above the ordinary failure. U-S4 is my attempt to correct for that, but I still have no public way to see a quiet supplier. I may overrate state interest in a scanner line; most incidents on the record were opportunistic.
