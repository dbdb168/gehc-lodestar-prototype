# Foreign trade and payments, phase 6a, round 1, summary

## Key points

A trade measure is the first mover in only one future, F3, where an entity listing on the China plant's logic supplier stops the plant within a quarter of the listing in the second half of 2027 and the stop lasts as long as the licence queue (N17, N10, N31; Panel judgement). In F4 a producer-country dual-use notice on cryocoolers and a parent exit together stop both plants by 2029 for most of 18 months, because controls are addressed by destination and the listing strands only the China plant (N05, N16, N17; Panel judgement). In F1 and F2 every tariff, customs delay and payment friction stays a cost inside the 30-day line, and the breaks belong to helium cover and the cold head (N04, N08, N31, N19, N05; Panel judgement). The shared encryption break lands through this lens on the port terminal and the payment rails, holding every export magnet for 35 days in all four futures and extending the licence queue in F3 (N26, N31; Panel judgement). Two Placeholders remove breaks: the China plant's logic stock in weeks, if longer than the licence queue, and the helium cover days, if above 60 (N17, N19; Placeholder).

## In one hundred words

Through this lens, rules break the line only on the stop side of the trade axis. F3 breaks the China plant through United States-origin logic in late 2027, the licence queue setting how long. F4 breaks the cold head by 2029 through a listing that closes the licence route plus a parent allocation. F1 and F2 carry no trade break; tariffs and rails are a cost. The encryption event at the port or rails holds export magnets 35 days everywhere. Confidence moderate on the F3 and F4 nodes, low on dates that rest on notices not yet written.
