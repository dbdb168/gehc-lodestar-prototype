# Natural disasters, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

Hurricane closures of the one export port are two to three days on the port authority's own logs (Dorian 2019, Florence 2018) and Helene 2024 did not close it at all, while the Pee Dee basin behind the US magnet plant has already had three 100-year rainfall events between 2015 and 2018 (N16, N24, N26; SC Ports Authority 2019 and 2024, SC State Climatology Office 2018).

Grid recovery after an inland storm in South Carolina ran about ten days after Helene, with the utility rebuilding rather than repairing, so power loss, not wind, is the second-order hazard for a plant that liquefies its own helium (N16, N19, N21; Duke Energy, 30 September 2024; plant dependence on grid is AI-suggested, Site operations to confirm).

Helium slack is gone for most of the horizon because Qatar's minister has stated three to five years of repair and up to five years of force majeure after the March 2026 strikes, so any weather outage at the now privately owned US Cliffside and Hugoton system meets a thinner buffer than in 2021 or 2022 (N01, N19, N23, N34; QatarEnergy statement 20 March 2026, BLM 27 June 2024, Physics Today 2022).

The hazard classifications over the Japanese cold-head source and the Tianjin plant have already moved: the Nankai Trough 30-year figure was revised to roughly 60 to 90 percent or higher in September 2025, and Tianjin is a named subsidence hotspot that saw an unexplained 80 to 160 centimetre Bohai surge in October 2024 (N05, N06, N03, N17, N29; Headquarters for Earthquake Research Promotion 2024, The Nation 2025, Science via Asia Times 2024, SCMP 2024; plant locations are AI-suggested, Engineering and Site operations to confirm).

The rainfall numbers US continuity plans are written against will be superseded before 2031, since NOAA Atlas 15 publishes nonstationary estimates no earlier than September 2027 and Wisconsin's 24-hour rainfall record fell in August 2025 (N16, N18, N24, N25; NOAA Office of Water Prediction, NOAA NCEI 19 December 2025).

## In one hundred words

Nothing in the natural hazard record says the line stops for 30 days by 2031. The port closes for days, the grid for about ten; both are rehearsed. What is settled is the baseline they land on: Qatar's helium is partly out until about 2029 to 2031, the Nankai and Tianjin classifications have been raised, and US design rainfall is about to be rewritten. The exposures I cannot see are the wire mill and the cold-head plant, whose addresses are missing from the map. The real uncertainty is coincidence: a US storm and a helium outage in the same month.
