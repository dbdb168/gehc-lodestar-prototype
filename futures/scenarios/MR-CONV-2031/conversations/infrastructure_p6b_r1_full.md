# Infrastructure, phase 6b, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line: the actions that pay in every future are the cheap ones that make the physical margins visible (A10) and tested (A9); the helium actions pay in the futures the read date leans toward. My ranking is A10, A9, A3.

Start by second quarter 2027. Too late after end 2027. Act if a 3 tesla sealed clearance appears in the FDA database. Pause if conventional builds still dominate orders in 2028. Look again if the maker's parent announces an MR exit. Cost of waiting: the source arrives after the gap opens. Panel judgement.

**A2 Multi-year agreement with the parent.** Verdict: a bet on F4. Protects N05 (R5). Why now: the last normal negotiation before a sale. Cost: contract minimums, low capital. Start by the 2027 cycle. Too late after a sale notice. Act if the parent's filings show the cryogenics unit under review. Pause if A1 qualifies. Look again if the unit changes hands. Cost of waiting: a year of exposure; a contract moves no part through a closed port (N30). Panel judgement.

**A3 Helium cover above hold plus grid rebuild.** Verdict: works in F1 and F3; a carrying cost in F2 and F4. Protects N19, N16, N21 (R8, R1). Too late after the 2028 hurricane season. Act if Hormuz transits stay below half of normal through the 2027 cycle. Pause if allocation letters stop and the Gulf reopens. Look again if the sealed test draw is a cylinder. Cost of waiting: one 30 to 45 day stop at N16 in winter 2028. Placeholder until Logistics states cover days.
