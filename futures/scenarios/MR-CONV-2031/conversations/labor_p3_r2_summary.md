## Key points

This lens keeps C1, restriction becomes a stop, as the first axis and replaces C2 with a merger of C2 and C3 worded as an outcome: in 2031, is most of what the OEM builds and ships still a helium-cooled conventional magnet, because a helium question that resolves through the Gulf changes no trade on the floor while one that resolves through sealing changes every trade (N16, N19, N20; Panel judgement; FDA K253779, 5 Feb 2026).
