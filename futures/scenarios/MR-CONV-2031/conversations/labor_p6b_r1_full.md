# Labor, phase 6b, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line: through the labour lens three actions work in every future, A10, A9 and A11, in that order, because a trade that retires cannot be bought back the way a part can. Every block is Panel judgement unless marked.

**A1 Second cold-head maker.** Verdict: works in F2 and F4. Protects N05 (R5), N20. Why now: the engineers who qualify a maker are the same small team converting the line, and 18 months must start in 2027. Cost and time: 18 months; money unknown. Start by second quarter 2027. Too late after end of 2027. Act if a 3 tesla sealed clearance appears in the FDA database. Pause if the sealed share of new builds stays below a fifth through 2028. Look again if the maker's parent announces an exit. Cost of waiting a year: qualification lands after the sealed share passes half.

**A2 Multi-year agreement with the parent.** Verdict: a bet on F4. Protects N05 (R5). Why now: the parent decides in the 2027 cycle. Cost and time: months; a premium. Start by the 2027 sourcing cycle. Too late after the parent's next portfolio review. Act if the parent discloses a review naming cryogenics. Pause if A1 lands. Look again if the parent is acquired. Cost of waiting a year: a year less notice. A contract does not keep a small workforce in one town at its benches. AI-suggested; Sourcing confirms the workforce size.

**A3 Helium cover above hold plus ten days.** Verdict: works in F1 and F3. Protects N19 (R8), N16 (R1). Why now: winter 2028. Cost and time: a quarter to fill; storage capital unknown. Start by second quarter 2027. Too late after autumn 2028. Act if an allocation letter arrives or Gulf transits stay below ten a week. Pause if Hormuz reopens to insured traffic. Look again if Engineering confirms a cylinder-sized sealed draw. Cost of waiting a year: one winter uncovered. Cover also absorbs a short strike at N26.

**A4 Liquefier on backup power.** Verdict: works in F1 and F3. Protects N16 (R1), N19 (R8). Why now: winter 2028. Cost and time: a year; low millions, a guess. Start by mid-2027. Too late after summer 2028. Act if the Carolinas rating is confirmed elevated for winter 2027. Pause if the utility signs a firm feeder contract. Look again if the site moves. Cost of waiting a year: the work competes with the grid rebuild for the same electrical trades.

**A5 Stock at the China plant.** Verdict: a bet on F3, cheap to hold in F1. Protects N17 (R2), N10 (R6), N05 (R5). Why now: the truce notice. Cost and time: weeks; working capital. **A6 Non-United States logic path.** Verdict: works in F3 and F4. Protects N10 (R6), N17 (R2). Why now: 18 months before a 2027 listing bites. Cost and time: 18 months; one engineering team. **A7 Thirty-day hold without a cold head.** Verdict: works in F2 and F4. Protects N05 (R5), N33. Why now: the sealed share passes half around 2029. Cost and time: two years; unknown. Start by 2027. **A8 Ramp and test capacity at the United States plant.** Verdict: works in F3 and F4. Protects N21, N16 (R1), N17 (R2). Why now: the 2027 capital plan. **A9 Thirty-day manual fallback.** Verdict: works in all four. Protects N16, N17, N03, N05 (R1, R2, R4, R5). Why now: no future removes the 35-day event. ## Critical factors

Time to competence for winding and cryogenic trades, the age profile at N20, and the contract calendar at the port and plants. None is public; all are Placeholders in effect.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: retirements already in the age curve and the 18-month training time. Uncertain: which trade the 2031 line needs (outcomes, settled by A11) and whether automation shortens competence (causation).

## Contradictions with other lenses

The panel voted nine to two that F2 breaks at N05, not N20. I hold the minority: in a sealed future the trades change faster than the parts, and a cold head nobody is qualified to integrate is the same stop. Human resources' age data would settle it.

## Blind spots of this lens

I may overrate the retirement cliff and underrate how fast a plant retrains under pressure. I see people and miss the automation that could make M1 unnecessary.
