## Critical factors

Time to competence for winding and cryogenic trades, the age profile at N20, and the contract calendar at the port and plants. None is public.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: retirements already in the age curve and the 18-month training time. Uncertain: which trade the 2031 line needs (outcomes) and whether automation shortens competence (causation).

## Contradictions with other lenses

The panel voted nine to two that F2 breaks at N05, not N20. I hold the minority: a cold head nobody is qualified to integrate is the same stop. Age data from Human resources would settle it.

## Blind spots of this lens

I may overrate the retirement cliff, underrate how fast a plant retrains under pressure, and miss the automation that could make M1 unnecessary.
