# Contrarian, phase 3, round 2, summary

## Key points

This lens accepts C1 as the first axis as written, because whether any trade measure becomes a stop rather than a cost moves four risks as breaks and one as cost (N17, N03, N05, N10, N18; Panel judgement).

The second axis is C2 with its sealed-magnet clause removed, since helium consumed at a sealed magnet's test is a Placeholder and the pace that makes it matter is the OEM's own decision, so the axis becomes whether helium is still an allocated market in 2031 or back to a buyers' market (N01, N17, N19, N27; Panel judgement; FDA K253779, 5 Feb 2026).

The two axes are independent in three corners and share one driver in the fourth, because a United States helium export rule is both a trade measure and an allocation (N19, N17; Global Trade Alert, 10 Jul 2026; Panel judgement).

C4 goes to phase 6a as a shared break on condition that every future states its hidden assumption that the design record is intact, C5 is a modifier on every future because it moves cost only, and C7 is a modifier on the two allocated-helium futures (N22, N36, N16, N18, N19; Panel judgement; NERC, 29 Jan 2026).

Round 1's claim that sealed adoption runs through the trade wall is withdrawn, because the Technology shift lens showed the OEM's pace is its own decision and the competitor's Shenzhen-built platforms move share, not the cold head or the cover (N36, N05, N19; Panel judgement).

## In one hundred words

The moderator's pair holds, with one change. Axis one is whether restriction becomes a stop. Axis two is whether helium is still allocated in 2031, with the sealed-magnet clause stripped out because it is a Placeholder plus a decision, and leaving it in lets the sponsor steer a future by choosing it. Three corners are independent; the fourth shares a driver. The encryption event goes to phase 6a as a shared break, provided every future admits it assumes the design record is intact. Demand shape modifies every future's bill. Grid curtailment modifies the allocated-helium futures. One round 1 claim is withdrawn.
