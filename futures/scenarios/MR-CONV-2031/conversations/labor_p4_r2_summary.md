# Labor, phase 4, round 2, summary

## Key points

In F2, where trade stays a cost and helium no longer decides, the first break is the skilled technicians at the United States magnet plant, because the sealed share passes half of output in 2029 before the second crew is 18 months into competence, and installs run more than 30 days late for two to three quarters without any line stop (N20, N16, N33; Panel judgement, low confidence). The cold head named by both cluster-mates is F4's break rather than F2's, since a parent exit is folded into U1 end B and only an unscheduled earthquake remains in this future (N05; Panel judgement). The action the world needs is a second crew trained from the second quarter of 2027 by Human resources with Site operations, and the sealed-line headcount is a Placeholder nobody has set (N20; Placeholder, Site operations). The 35-day encryption event stops the line past 30 days and restarts slower here because an idled crew mid-conversion is hired away (N16, N20, N22; Panel judgement). Automation of leak test and cold-head integration would remove this break, which is the blind spot this lens carries (N20; Panel judgement).

## In one hundred words

F2 is the comfortable future, and its only break is on the floor. No part stops, helium leaves the tolerance story, and the plant converts most of its output to sealed magnets while swapping one 18-month trade for another on a roster whose age nobody has confirmed. The sealed share passes half in 2029 before the second crew is competent, and installs run late for two to three quarters. The cluster's cold-head break belongs to F4 by the panel's own definition. Confidence is low: it rests on time to competence, roster age and the absence of automation, all unconfirmed.
