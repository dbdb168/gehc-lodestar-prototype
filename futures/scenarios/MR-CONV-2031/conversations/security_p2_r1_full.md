# Security, phase 2, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line first. Three things are already fixed to 2031. The rules are written, with dates in the United States, the European Union, Japan and China that mostly fall before the 2027 sourcing cycle. The observed recovery time for an encrypted manufacturer is longer than the 30-day tolerance line. The counterfeit and cargo-theft markets that matter to this product are measured. Who gets hit, and when, is left for phase 3.

**Element 1: cyber duties on the product are law.** Section 524B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act took effect about 29 March 2023. A "cyber device" sponsor must file a postmarket vulnerability plan, keep a patching process, and provide a software bill of materials covering commercial, open-source and off-the-shelf components ([21 U.S.C. 360n-2](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/360n-2), enacted 29 December 2022). The FDA's final guidance is dated February 2026 and supersedes the 27 June 2025 version ([FDA guidance page](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/cybersecurity-medical-devices-quality-management-system-considerations-and-content-premarket), 3 February 2026). This touches N15 and N22 directly and N08 to N12 indirectly, since every firmware-bearing board becomes a line in the bill of materials. Only repeal would unmake it. That the OEM's filings already cover bought-in boards is AI-suggested; Quality and regulatory can confirm.

**Element 2: the European Union has dated duties on suppliers and on the OEM.** The Cyber Resilience Act applies from 11 December 2027, its Article 14 reporting duties from 11 September 2026, and it excludes products under the medical device regulation ([Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Articles 2(2)(a) and 71](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj), 20 November 2024; [NCSC Ireland](https://ncsc.gov.ie/cra/), read 11 October 2026). I interpret the exclusion as narrow: the scanner is out, but the amplifiers, logic and memory their makers sell into the EU are in, so the makers behind N08 to N12 carry a 24-hour reporting duty from September 2026. NIS2 names medical device manufacturers as covered entities with supply-chain duties; transposition was due 17 October 2024 ([DLA Piper, 4 December 2024](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/blogs/cortex-life-sciences-insights/2024/nis2-series-part-2)). This touches N15, N22 and N28. The Commission's July 2026 guidance confirms the dates ([Jones Day, July 2026](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/07/eu-cyber-resilience-act-24hour-reporting-duties-start-september-11-2026)). Whether the wire mills behind N03 fall under NIS2 is AI-suggested; Sourcing can ask.

**Element 3: the export port is under a dated cyber rule.** The Coast Guard rule took effect 16 July 2025 and requires regulated facilities to complete a cyber assessment and submit a cyber plan by 16 July 2027, with immediate incident reporting ([Jones Walker via National Law Review, 28 January 2025](https://natlawreview.com/article/coast-guard-issues-final-maritime-cybersecurity-rule-key-requirements-and)). The extension consulted on covers vessels, not facilities. This touches N26. The precedent is Nagoya, where ransomware in the terminal system on 4 July 2023 stopped container handling for about two days ([BleepingComputer, 5 July 2023](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-largest-port-stops-operations-after-ransomware-attack/)). Panel judgement: a two-day port stop sits inside the tolerance line.

**Element 4: incident visibility is mandatory and fast.** Listed companies in the United States file within four business days of a materiality finding, since 18 December 2023 ([SEC, 26 July 2023](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-139)). Japan's Cyber Response Capability Strengthening Acts, passed 16 May 2025, make reporting mandatory for designated operators from 1 October 2026 ([Recorded Future](https://www.recordedfuture.com/research/japan-cyber-defense-strategy), read 11 October 2026). China's amended Cybersecurity Law took effect 1 January 2026 with higher fines and wider extraterritorial reach ([Mayer Brown, 15 December 2025](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2025/12/china-finalises-amendments-to-the-cybersecurity-law-what-businesses-need-to-know-before-1-january-2026)). The federal CIRCIA rule is the exception: the statute fixes 72-hour incident and 24-hour ransom clocks, but the final rule is unissued ([CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/cyber-threats-and-advisories/information-sharing/cyber-incident-reporting-critical-infrastructure-act-2022-circia), read 11 October 2026; [Hunton, 17 July 2026](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/cisa-plans-to-finalize-cyber-incident-reporting-regulations-in-september-2026)). This touches N16, N17, N18 and the listed suppliers behind N03, N05 and N08 to N12. Panel judgement: a supplier incident becomes public in days, before tender bodies on N36 read it.

**Element 5: cryptography has a dated retirement.** NIST's draft plan deprecates 112-bit RSA, ECDSA and EdDSA after 2030 and disallows all quantum-vulnerable schemes after 2035 ([NIST IR 8547 draft, Table 2](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2024/NIST.IR.8547.ipd.pdf), November 2024). The 2030 date is inside the horizon and touches N15, N22 and N31. Moderate confidence, because the report is still a draft.

**Element 6: the console operating system clock is fixed.** Windows 10 IoT Enterprise LTSC 2021 reaches end of support on 14 January 2032, just past the horizon; mainstream Windows 10 ended 14 October 2025 ([Microsoft lifecycle](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/windows-10-iot-enterprise-ltsc-2021), read 11 October 2026). Which edition the consoles run is AI-suggested; Engineering confirms. Touches N15 and N11.

**Element 7: the counterfeit market for control electronics is measured.** Programmable logic was the most reported counterfeit part type in 2025 at more than 15 percent of reports, and one FPGA brand was again the most targeted; total reports fell to 748, which ERAI reads as trend ([ERAI 2025 annual report](https://www.erai.com/erai_blog/3192/2025_annual_report), read 11 October 2026). Touches N10 directly. The link to N11 and N12 through memory allocation and grey-market modules rests on trade sources only ([Findchips, 2026](https://blog.findchips.com/dram-memory-shortage-2026-pricing-lead-times-where-to-buy/)), so low confidence.

**Element 8: cargo theft is high and aimed at electronics and deception.** Verisk CargoNet counted 2,646 confirmed thefts in 2025, up 18 percent, with losses near $725 million, and expects RAM, storage and enterprise computing to be targeted in 2026 ([SDCE summary of the Verisk release, 2026](https://www.sdcexec.com/safety-security/regulations/news/22959008/cargonet-cargo-theft-losses-in-2025-experience-60-increase-from-2024-verisk-cargonet-analysis)). Strategic theft by impersonation and fictitious carriers was about 30 percent of US incidents ([BSI and Munich Re via Risk and Insurance, 1 July 2026](https://riskandinsurance.com/strategic-cargo-theft-now-accounts-for-nearly-a-third-of-all-us-incidents-report-finds/)), and electronics was the most targeted product type ([Overhaul 2025 report](https://www.over-haul.com/reports/united-states-canada-annual-cargo-theft-report-2025), read 11 October 2026). Touches N30, N24, N25 and N31. Panel judgement: an oversize magnet is not a theft target; the inbound boards and coils on N30 are.

**Element 9: recovery from encryption runs past the tolerance line.** A vehicle maker announced an attack on 2 September 2025, restarted in phases by 8 October, booked £196 million in the quarter, and needed a £1.5 billion state-backed guarantee to pay suppliers ([BleepingComputer, 15 November 2025](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/jaguar-land-rover-cyberattack-cost-the-company-over-220-million/); [UK government, 28 September 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-backs-jaguar-land-rover-with-15-billion-loan-guarantee)). A brewer attacked in September 2025 had production at 30 plants disrupted and reporting systems out for months ([Food Ingredients First, 18 March 2026](https://www.foodingredientsfirst.com/news/asahi-cyberattack-food-ransomware-crisis.html)). I infer that a full encryption event at N16, N17 or N18, or at the single mills behind N03 and N05, runs five weeks or longer. This would be wrong if the plant or mill has a tested manual fallback for winding and test records, which no public source can show.

## Critical factors

The single points of data at N22 and N15 carry this lens, because every replacement time on the map assumes those records are intact. The single-source mills at N03 and N05 follow, because their recovery time is the line's. Then the inbound electronics on N30 and N31, where theft and impersonation concentrate, and the control logic at N10, the class with the deepest counterfeit market.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the nine elements above. Uncertain, for phase 3 and not analysed here: whether a mill behind N03 or N05 is encrypted inside the horizon (outcome); whether CIRCIA is finalised and covers N16 and N18 (outcome); whether state pre-positioning in US infrastructure reaches N16 or N18 or their utilities (causation); whether counterfeit logic reaches N10 through authorised distribution (causation); whether strategic theft moves to project cargo on N25 and N26 (outcome); whether an insider or trusted supplier quietly degrades the record at N22 (causation); whether impersonation of brokers and banks on N31 becomes routine (outcome); whether China's extraterritorial powers are used against a foreign OEM's N17 in a trade dispute (value and outcome); whether a cryptographically relevant quantum computer arrives before 2035 (outcome).

## Contradictions with other lenses

Round 1 has no other summaries to read. The likely contradiction is with the node map's note that a cyber event at N15 is "a security exposure, not a supply one". This lens reads it as a supply event once N22 is included. What settles it is whether the plant's test and qualification systems can run with N15 and N22 unavailable.

## Blind spots of this lens

I see adversaries first and ordinary failure second. The most damaging path may be a trusted supplier at N03 or N05 cutting corners for years, which no advisory flags. I may also overrate attacker interest in a niche product; most incidents in the record were opportunistic.
