# Customer and market, phase 3, round 2, summary

## Key points

This lens accepts C1, restriction becomes a stop, as axis 1, because from the buyer's chair it decides which plant may serve which market and pushes all United States demand onto the single hub if a scanner tariff lands (N17, N18, N28, N29; Federal Register, 26 Sep 2025).

Axis 2 is C2 reworded to absorb the buyer's half of C3: does helium still decide whether a magnet ships in 2031, given the Gulf route, any allocation rule, test consumption of a sealed magnet, and whether tenders require sealed designs; the OEM's own conversion pace is an action for phase 6b (N19, N01, N05, N36; Panel judgement; FDA K253779, 5 Feb 2026).

The two axes are independent in all four combinations, and this lens disagrees with the contrarian lens on that point because sealed demand from European and British tenders does not run through the trade wall, settled by whether any 2027 European tender requires a sealed design regardless of origin (N36, N17; Panel judgement).

C4 is a shared break for phase 6a, because a committee never asks why a magnet is late; C5 splits into a volume modifier on every future and a watch item on whether late delivery is scored at all; C7 is a watch item that modifies the two futures where helium stays a risk (N36, N35, N16, N18, N19; Panel judgement; NERC, 29 Jan 2026).

Correction to round 1: the sealed-magnet question is narrower than this lens wrote, since the contrarian and technology shift lenses showed the decision is half made, leaving only the buyer's pull as external (N05, N19, N21; FDA K253779, 5 Feb 2026).

## In one hundred words

The moderator's axes hold from the buyer's side. Restriction becoming a stop decides which plant serves which market. Helium still deciding shipment, with tender pull on sealed designs folded in, decides whether a committed install date survives a shortage. The two are independent in every combination. An encryption event is a shared break in every future, not an axis, because the committee never asks why. Demand shape modifies each future; whether late delivery is scored at all is a watch item on tender criteria. Grid curtailment is a watch item that bites only where helium stays a risk.
