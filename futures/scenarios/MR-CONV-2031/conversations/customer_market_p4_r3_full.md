The one thing missing is at N36. Every page converts a late install into lost share as if that were automatic. It is not. Share moves only where a competitor keeps delivering while the OEM is late. In F3 a competitor outside the listing bloc keeps shipping to Japan and India, so share moves. In F1 every maker's helium came through the same strait, in F4 a cryocooler or wire listing reaches every maker, and in F2 the whole sealed class warms up, so the market is late together and committees delay the tender rather than switch. The pages overstate the share cost in F1, F2 and F4 and understate how singular F3 is. Panel judgement, moderate; settled by whether competitors share the cold-head and wire sources (Sourcing, from public supplier disclosures) and whether any public tender has scored delivery performance, still a watch item.
