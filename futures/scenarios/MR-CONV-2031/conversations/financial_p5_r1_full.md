## Analysis

Bottom line: the money side of U1 and U2 is already published in four places, and two of them can be read by a machine today. Owner exit from the wire mill shows up first in a listed parent's filings, not in a trade notice. Whether helium still decides in 2031 shows up in the bulk price by region and in whether marine war-risk underwriters still list the Gulf. Nothing here needs building.

**Id:** FIN-3. **Uncertainty:** U2. **Name:** Bulk helium price by region. **What is measured:** the quarterly contract price per tonne of bulk helium in the United States, Germany, France, Qatar and India, and whether buyers are inside allocation frameworks (N01, N19, N34). **Source name:** IMARC bulk helium price index. **Source link:** https://www.imarcgroup.com/news/bulk-helium-price-index (confirmed 11 October 2026). **Cadence:** quarterly. **Mode:** manual; a web page, not a feed. The yearly check is the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries helium chapter, https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-helium.pdf (February 2026). **Settles toward A when:** the United States price stays above $100,000 per tonne through 2027 and the index still describes allocation rather than spot. **Settles toward B when:** two consecutive quarters fall toward the USGS Grade-A base of about $12 per cubic metre with no surcharge, and the index reports spot availability. **False signal:** a price fall caused by hospitals and fabs cutting demand in a recession, not by supply returning; the Gulf transit count separates the two. **Last read:** Q1 2026 prices were $102,597 per tonne in the United States, $123,727 in Germany, $127,738 in France and $97,240 in Qatar ([IMARC, 20 May 2026](https://www.imarcgroup.com/news/bulk-helium-price-index)). **Leans toward:** a.

**Id:** FIN-4. **Uncertainty:** U2. **Name:** Gulf marine war-risk listing. **What is measured:** whether the London market's Joint War Committee keeps the Persian/Arabian Gulf and Qatar on its listed areas, which sets the additional premium every helium container ship pays and whether a voyage is insurable at all (N01, N23, N27). **Source name:** Lloyd's Market Association, Joint War Committee listed areas. **Source link:** https://lmalloyds.com/committee/joint-war-committee/ (confirmed 11 October 2026). **Cadence:** reviewed at least quarterly, circulars as events occur. **Mode:** manual; circulars are PDFs. **Settles toward A when:** the Gulf and Qatar stay listed through 2028 and underwriters review voyages case by case. **Settles toward B when:** a circular removes Qatar and the Gulf from the list and the Hormuz transit count returns toward 85 a day. **False signal:** a boundary redraw that shrinks the zone while Qatar stays listed; the premium still applies to the helium lane. **Last read:** JWLA-033 of 3 March 2026 added Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain; JWLA-034 of 29 July 2026 amended the Gulf zone; the committee's page cites JWLA-035 and a review in September 2026 ([LMA, Sep 2026](https://lmalloyds.com/committee/joint-war-committee/); [Seatrade, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.seatrade-maritime.com/security/london-marine-insurers-extend-middle-east-war-risk-zones)). **Leans toward:** a.
