# Labor, phase 6b, round 1, summary

## Key points

The three actions that work in all four futures are A10 (confirm the Placeholders), A9 (the thirty-day manual fallback) and A11 (decide the pace of conversion to sealed magnets), ranked in that order by the cost of waiting a year, because the people who could write a manual procedure or train a new trade retire on a clock no future moves (N16, N17, N20; Panel judgement).

A9 is the labour lens's core case: a manual fallback is tacit knowledge, it leaves with the people who last ran the line on paper, and the act-if reading is whether those technicians are within two years of retirement (N16, N17, N03, N05; Panel judgement).

A11 binds in 2027 because a sealed line and a conventional line need different trades and 18 months to competence means hiring must begin before the capital plan closes, so the decision is also a hiring decision (N20, N05, N19; Panel judgement).

Two actions are missing from the candidate list: Labor M1, build the trade pipeline at the US plant before the conversion decision, and Labor M2, map contract expiries at the plants, the port and the oversize carriers against hurricane and winter windows (N20, N16, N18, N26, N25; AI-suggested, Human resources and Logistics confirm).

A8, ramp and test capacity at the US plant, is empty without M1, because capacity is technicians rather than floor space, and A2, the agreement with the cold-head parent, is weak through this lens because a contract does not keep a small workforce at its benches (N21, N16, N05; Panel judgement).

## In one hundred words

Through the labour lens, A10, A9 and A11 work in every future and rank in that order by the cost of waiting. A9 matters most to this lens: the thirty-day manual fallback is tacit knowledge that leaves when the people who ran the line on paper retire. A11 is a hiring decision in disguise, because sealed and conventional lines need different trades and competence takes 18 months. Two actions are missing: a trade pipeline at the US plant before the conversion decision, and a contract calendar for the plants, port and carriers set against the storm and winter windows.
