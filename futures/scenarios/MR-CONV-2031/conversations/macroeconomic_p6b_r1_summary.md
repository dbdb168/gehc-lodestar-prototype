# Macroeconomic, phase 6b, round 1, summary

## Key points

Confirming the Placeholders costs nothing and sizes every other action, so it ranks first and works in all four futures (N19, N16, N05, N03, N17; Placeholder). The manual fallback test is the second action that works in all four, because the shared break reaches the plants and sole suppliers regardless of trade or helium (N16, N17, N18, N03, N05; Panel judgement). Helium cover above the hold plus grid rebuild ranks third because its break arrives first, winter 2028 to 2029, and cover cannot be bought at allocated volumes once the shortage bites (N19, N16, N21; Placeholder). Two missing actions: fix helium allocation priority in writing and hedge yen exposure on cold heads (N01, N34, N05, N06; Panel judgement).

## In one hundred words

Price does not clear a shortage in time, so this lens ranks actions by how many futures they cover and how much a year of delay costs. Confirm the Placeholders now, test the manual fallback in 2027, and build helium cover before the 2028 winter. The second cold-head maker and the sealed hold work in the two sealed futures; the China stock, logic redesign and Asian test capacity are bets on a stop.
