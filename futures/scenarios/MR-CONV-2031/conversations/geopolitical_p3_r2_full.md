Four of my round 1 uncertainties are directions on C1; the helium question is a direction on C2. ## Blind spots of this lens

I still count capability as action; the 2017 Qatar blockade and the Russian helium licence show states holding a lever without pulling it, so the "stop" end of C1 may never land by 2031. I discount the domestic politics that decide the Section 232 outcome and the truce.
