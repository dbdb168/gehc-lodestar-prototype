# Natural disasters, phase 5, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line first. Hazards do not resolve the axes by themselves; they decide whether a future already leaning one way breaks early. ND1 can push U1 toward end B through the cold-head source. ND2 and ND3 watch the one lane a storm can stop. ND4 reads whether the magnet plant's grid turns a helium delay into a stop, end A of U2 by the back door. Research was confirmation only, all on 11 October 2026.

**Id** ND1. **Uncertainty** U1. **Name** Nankai Trough standing evaluation. **What is measured** The official 30-year probability of a magnitude 8 to 9 earthquake on the trough. **Source name** Headquarters for Earthquake Research Promotion, Japan. **Source link** https://www.jishin.go.jp/main/chousa/24apr_bungo/p18h-e.htm. **Cadence** Annual revision. **Mode** Manual; the English page is static. **Settles toward A when** The horizon passes with no rupture and Sourcing confirms the cold-head plant (N05) stands outside the shaking and tsunami zones. **Settles toward B when** A rupture occurs with the plant inside the damage zone; a sole source stopped more than 30 days by a hazard is end B whatever trade does. **False signal** A temporary advisory after a magnitude 7 foreshock, as in August 2024, that lapses in a week. **Last read** The page still carries 70 to 80 percent over 30 years, dated around April 2024. **Leans toward** Neither. Nodes N05, N03, N06. Location AI-suggested; Sourcing confirms.

**Id** ND2. **Uncertainty** U1. **Name** Export port closure log. **What is measured** Days the harbour terminals (N26) are closed, by advisory. **Source name** The port authority's advisories page. **Source link** https://scspa.com/advisories/. **Cadence** Per event, daily in a storm. **Mode** Live; a dated, machine-readable page without a feed. **Settles toward A when** Every season to 2031 produces closures of two to three days, the pattern since 2018. **Settles toward B when** One harbour closure exceeds 30 days, which no entry on the record shows. **False signal** An inland-yard or gate-hours notice; the 11 April 2026 Greer closure is an inland yard. **Last read** Page one on 11 October 2026 lists no weather closure in 2026; the latest entry is a gate-hours notice of 23 September 2026. **Leans toward** A. Nodes N26, N24, N25.

**Id** ND3. **Uncertainty** U1. **Name** Atlantic cyclone feed. **What is measured** Active cyclones, tracks and formation outlook. **Source name** National Hurricane Center. **Source link** https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/index-at.xml. **Cadence** Six-hourly in season. **Mode** Live; RSS already read by the command centre's disaster panel. **Settles toward A when** Landfalls leave the port and plant on their rehearsed two to ten day recovery. **Settles toward B when** A major hurricane lands between port and plant and ND2 then records a closure beyond 30 days; this feed never settles the axis alone. **False signal** A post-tropical system with inland rain that closes roads for days; the September 2024 grid recovery was ten days, not thirty. **Last read** Feed built 11 October 2026, 18:01 GMT: a post-tropical cyclone inland with flood risk in the Carolinas, final advisory issued, no active cyclone, one disturbance at 20 percent. **Leans toward** A. Nodes N16, N26, N24.

**Id** ND4. **Uncertainty** U2. **Name** Winter grid rating at the plant's region. **What is measured** The risk rating and extreme-scenario reserve margin for the regions covering N16 (SERC-East) and N18 (MISO). **Source name** North American Electric Reliability Corporation, Winter Reliability Assessment. **Source link** https://prod.nerc.com/globalassets/our-work/assessments/nerc_wra_2025.pdf, found by search and opened. **Cadence** Annual, each November. **Mode** Manual; a PDF read each quarter against the plant's backup-power Placeholder. **Settles toward A when** The region is rated high and a multi-week curtailment hits the plant while the Gulf is still impaired, because the liquefier stops and helium cover (N19) becomes the margin. **Settles toward B when** It cannot; a normal rating only removes one route to end A. **False signal** A cold-snap advisory that lifts in days. **Last read** November 2025 edition: SERC-East winter peak up 700 megawatts with firm capacity down, now potentially winter-peaking, some loss-of-load hours; MISO reserve falls to 3.7 percent in the extreme scenario. **Leans toward** A, weakly. Nodes N16, N19, N21, N18.

Live today: ND3 in the disaster panel, and ND2 as a polled page. Quarterly by a person: ND1 and ND4.

## Critical factors

The lane and the grid, not the roof. ND2 and ND4 measure what the plant depends on; ND1 measures a classification over a plant nobody has placed.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: closure and grid rebuild durations. Uncertain: a Nankai rupture inside the horizon (outcomes); whether a curtailment reaches the liquefier (causation, until Site operations sets backup power); where N05 stands (actions).

## Contradictions with other lenses

None read this round. Carried: other lenses own the Gulf and allocation readings that settle U2.

## Blind spots of this lens

No indicator for the Tianjin plant (N17); I could not confirm a readable Bohai surge warning address and will not invent one. Slow hazards have no row.
