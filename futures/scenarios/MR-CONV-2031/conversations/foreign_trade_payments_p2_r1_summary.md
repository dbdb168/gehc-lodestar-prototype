# Foreign trade and payments, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

Qatar supplied about a third of world helium in 2025, its Ras Laffan trains were struck on 18 March 2026, the producer says repairs take three to five years, and the Strait of Hormuz was still effectively closed on 9 October 2026, so the helium gap runs into the 2029 to 2031 window. N01, N19, N21, N23, N27, N34. USGS, February 2026; Industrial Info, 26 March 2026; straits.live, 9 October 2026.

Russian helium is under export licensing until 31 December 2027 and Chinese helium is under an export ban with no end date, on top of the EU and United States bans on Russian helium, so neither producer can backfill the United States plant while the Chinese ban keeps domestic helium available to the China plant. N01, N17, N19, N34. Interfax, 14 April 2026; Global Trade Alert interventions 154600 and 157329; European Commission, 24 June 2024.

United States import duties on wire, cold heads, logic, memory and graphics now rest on Section 232 and Section 301 measures with no expiry: 10 percent combined for the EU, United Kingdom, Taiwan and Canada and 12.5 percent for Japan, South Korea, China and Brazil from 24 July 2026, after the Supreme Court ended emergency-power tariffs on 20 February 2026. N03, N05, N08, N10, N11, N12, N31. EY, July 2026; K&L Gates, 20 February 2026.

Magnet-grade niobium-titanium wire in a copper matrix sits outside the 50 percent copper tariff annex and outside the EU, Wassenaar and Chinese control lists, so it crosses borders under ordinary duties today. N03, N17. Federal Register, 5 August 2025; Trade Hub 1C005; Clark Hill, October 2025. AI-suggested; Sourcing confirms the declared classification.

Niobium stays 93 percent Brazilian with the United States wholly import dependent, the USMCA now sits on annual review to 2036, and China's procurement rule of 6 July 2025 rewards devices made inside China. N02, N17, N29. USGS, February 2026; White and Case, July 2026; CTILS, July 2025.

## In one hundred words

The border rules for this line are largely written. Helium is the predetermined shock: Qatar's third of world supply is out for years, Hormuz is still shut, and Russia and China have locked their helium in by decree, so cover at the United States plant is tested monthly. United States duties on imported components have moved to Section 232 and Section 301 with no sunset, at 10 or 12.5 percent by partner. Niobium-titanium wire is uncontrolled and outside the copper tariff. Open for phase 3: a device tariff, mineral price floors, the copper phase-in, wider chip tariffs, the China truce.
