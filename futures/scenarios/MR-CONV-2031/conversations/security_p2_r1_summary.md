# Security, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

Cyber duties on the product and its makers are already law with dates inside the horizon: section 524B in the United States since March 2023 with final guidance in February 2026, the EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting duties from 11 September 2026 and full application from 11 December 2027 for the bought-in electronics, the Coast Guard port cyber plan deadline of 16 July 2027, Japan's mandatory reporting from 1 October 2026, and China's amended Cybersecurity Law from 1 January 2026 (N15, N22, N08 to N12, N26, N17; [Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21/360n-2), [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj), [National Law Review, 28 January 2025](https://natlawreview.com/article/coast-guard-issues-final-maritime-cybersecurity-rule-key-requirements-and), [Recorded Future](https://www.recordedfuture.com/research/japan-cyber-defense-strategy), [Mayer Brown, 15 December 2025](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2025/12/china-finalises-amendments-to-the-cybersecurity-law-what-businesses-need-to-know-before-1-january-2026)).

The observed recovery time for a manufacturer that is encrypted is five weeks or longer, which is past the 30-day tolerance line, so a full encryption event at a plant or at either single-source mill is a line stop, not an IT problem (N16, N17, N18, N03, N05; [BleepingComputer, 15 November 2025](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/jaguar-land-rover-cyberattack-cost-the-company-over-220-million/), [Food Ingredients First, 18 March 2026](https://www.foodingredientsfirst.com/news/asahi-cyberattack-food-ransomware-crisis.html); the inference to this line is Panel judgement).

Programmable logic devices were the most reported counterfeit part class in 2025 at more than 15 percent of reports, so the control electronics are the component with the deepest documented counterfeit market on this map (N10, with N11 and N12 under allocation; [ERAI 2025 annual report](https://www.erai.com/erai_blog/3192/2025_annual_report)).

Cargo theft rose to 2,646 confirmed incidents in 2025 with losses near $725 million, about 30 percent by impersonation or fraudulent pickup, and electronics the most targeted product type, so the exposure is the inbound boards and coils rather than the oversize magnet (N30, N24, N25, N31; [Verisk CargoNet via SDCE, 2026](https://www.sdcexec.com/safety-security/regulations/news/22959008/cargonet-cargo-theft-losses-in-2025-experience-60-increase-from-2024-verisk-cargonet-analysis), [Risk and Insurance, 1 July 2026](https://riskandinsurance.com/strategic-cargo-theft-now-accounts-for-nearly-a-third-of-all-us-incidents-report-finds/)).

The 2030 deprecation of 112-bit RSA and elliptic-curve signatures falls inside the horizon and reaches the software, the design records and the payment rails, held at moderate confidence because the NIST plan is still a draft (N15, N22, N31; [NIST IR 8547 draft, November 2024](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2024/NIST.IR.8547.ipd.pdf)).

## In one hundred words

The security rules that bind this line by 2031 are already written, with most dates before the 2027 sourcing cycle: United States device cyber law, the EU Cyber Resilience Act for bought-in electronics, a port cyber plan deadline in July 2027, and new reporting regimes in Japan and China. The recovery record is plain: an encrypted manufacturer takes five weeks or more to restart, past the tolerance line, and the single mills behind wire and cold heads carry the same clock. Counterfeit programmable logic and electronics cargo theft are measured markets. Who is hit, and when, is for phase 3.
