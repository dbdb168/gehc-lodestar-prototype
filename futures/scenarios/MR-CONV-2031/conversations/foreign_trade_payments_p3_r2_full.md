# Foreign trade and payments, phase 3, round 2

## Analysis

Bottom line: this lens accepts the moderator's axes, C1 and C2. For a product owner: "By 2031, does any trade measure stop a part this magnet needs, or does every measure stay a cost and a customs delay inside 30 days?" and "In 2031, does helium still decide whether a magnet ships?"

C1 is where this lens's round 1 list collapses: a listing of wire or cold heads and a United States and China rupture are the stop end; tariffs, the copper annex and payment rails are the cost end. The technology shift lens sharpened the stop end: the likelier route to a listing is a quantum or fusion control sweeping in cryocoolers and niobium-titanium wire (N03, N05, N17; Panel judgement).

C2 holds the helium fences, which do not fit C1 because they move the two plants in opposite directions: Russia's licensing lapses on 31 December 2027, China's ban has no end date, and a United States export rule on helium would reverse the plants' positions (N16, N17, N19; Global Trade Alert, 14 April and 10 July 2026). C1 is an outcomes uncertainty; C2 is outcomes for the strait and causation for a sealed magnet's helium use at test.

The contrarian lens is right that C3 runs through C1, since a competitor's helium-light platforms are built in Shenzhen for the United States market (N17, N29), so C3 stays off the axes and inside C2 as a cause.

## Critical factors

Independence. Stop with helium a risk: a Japanese listing of cold heads lands while Hormuz stays shut (N05, N17, N19). Stop with helium not a risk: sealed magnets take the new builds but are still wound from listed wire (N03, N16, N17). Cost with helium a risk: the truce rolls over and the Gulf stays closed, so helium is bought on allocation (N01, N19, N31). Cost with helium not a risk: open trade and sealed conversion leave sites, labour and the design record as the breaks (N16, N18, N20, N22).

B moves whether the risk breaks; C moves only what the break costs.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

C4 is a shared break every future carries (phase 6a): an encryption event is a yes-or-no event, not a structure. C5 is a modifier on every future's bill and a watch item on tender criteria (N35, N36); it moves consequence, not likelihood. C7 is a modifier on the two futures where helium stays a line risk, since curtailment reaches the line only through the cover (N19).

## Contradictions with other lenses

The later source is more likely current, so this lens withdraws the November date; the Federal Register and MOFCOM notices settle it (N17, N31).
