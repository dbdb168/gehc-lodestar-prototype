# Technology shift, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

The sealed, helium-light 1.5 tesla magnet is a cleared product with service history at three large makers including the OEM, first cleared 3 Aug 2018 and cleared for the OEM's own platform on 5 Feb 2026, so by 2031 helium is an installed-base risk more than a new-build risk (N01, N19, N21, N23, N34; FDA K181479, 3 Aug 2018, and K253779, 5 Feb 2026; AI-suggested for the OEM's market coverage, Quality and regulatory to confirm).

Sealed magnets are still wound from niobium-titanium wire and still cooled by a Gifford-McMahon cold head, so the single wire mill and the single cold head source survive the shift and the cold head matters more without a helium bath to buffer an outage (N03, N05, N06, N02; Panel judgement).

Helium supply is structurally tighter for the horizon: Qatar supplied 63 of 190 million cubic metres in 2025, Ras Laffan was knocked out on 2 Mar 2026 with full repair estimated at three to five years, Russian helium is sanctioned, and the US sold its reserve on 27 Jun 2024 (N01, N19, N23, N34; USGS Feb 2026, PIIE 21 Apr 2026, BLM 27 Jun 2024).

No 1.5 or 3 tesla magnet without niobium-titanium wire appears in US clearance records to October 2026, and with 18 months to qualify a mill a wire change cannot reach a tenth of new installs by 2031 (N03, N22; openFDA read 11 Oct 2026; Panel judgement).

Dated rules and chip trends already in motion reach the electronics and the design record: EU legacy cover ends 31 Dec 2028, the Cyber Resilience Act applies 11 Dec 2027, mature-node capacity moves partly out of Taiwan by 2028, and legacy DRAM is on allocation (N10, N11, N12, N15, N22; EU 2023/607, EU 2024/2847, Counterpoint via Benzinga 23 Jan 2026, RAND Technology 19 Nov 2025).

## In one hundred words

The biggest change to this product by 2031 is already cleared for sale. Sealed 1.5 tesla magnets holding a few litres of helium are cleared products at three makers, including the OEM since February 2026. For new builds that shrinks the helium nodes; for the installed base helium stays a service risk, and supply is tighter after the Ras Laffan outage, Russian sanctions and the sale of the US reserve. The wire and cold head stay, and the cold head becomes more critical. Dated EU and US rules and the memory and mature-node shifts reach the electronics first.
