# Foreign trade and payments, phase 4, round 3, summary

## Key points

For F1 this lens adopts Natural disasters' helium cover break, because the only trade instrument that could reach a plant in that future sits on U2 and an allocation letter is a contract term, not a rule (N19, N17; Panel judgement). For F2 it adopts Technology shift's cold head through a hazard only, since a parent allocating output elsewhere is the owner-exit cause phase 3 folded into U1 end B and belongs in F4 (N05, N20; Panel judgement). For F3 it keeps the China plant stopped through United States-origin logic, with Security's payment freeze a quarter earlier if a data demand comes first (N17, N10, N31; Panel judgement, moderate). For F4 it adopts Macroeconomic's cold head over the Contrarian's wire, because a listing is a licence requirement that an allied producer normally grants to a United States plant, and only an owner's choice stops a part (N05, N03; Panel judgement, low on date). Macroeconomic's F4 page is internally inconsistent: a quarter lost at the China plant on a 2027 logic listing is itself a breach, so its first break would be N17 in 2027 (N17, N10; Panel judgement).
