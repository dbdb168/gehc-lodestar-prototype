# Macroeconomic, phase 6a, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line: both plants (R1 N16, R2 N17) break in all four futures, never on a macroeconomic cause. Money sets the bill and the date; a container, a cold head or a listing sets the break. Price is the mechanism only in F4, and that break rests on an unset Placeholder.

**F1, cost only, helium still decides.** Helium cover (R8 N19) breaks in the winter of 2028 to 2029. Cover is inventory, thinned each budget round at a 4 percent policy rate with producer prices rising about 5 percent a year (Federal Reserve, 16 September 2026). A winter curtailment then takes the liquefier inside a container hold window and the United States plant (R1 N16) stops 30 to 45 days, a breach. The China plant (R2 N17) breaks in the first half of 2028, buying Asian spot at the back of an allocated queue; Panel judgement. Confidence low to moderate: the curtailment path has no record and cover days are a Placeholder; cover above 60 days removes the United States break.

**F2, cost only, helium no longer decides.** The cold head (R5 N05) breaks between 2029 and 2031 by hazard at the sole maker. This lens supplies no cause, only the cost: both plants stop longer than 30 days because 18 months of requalification outlasts inventory (COCIR, 2023). Technicians (R7 N20) cost lateness, not a stop. Confidence moderate on the node, low on the date.

**F3, a part stops, helium still decides.** The China plant (R2 N17) breaks through logic (R6 N10) in the second half of 2027, within a quarter of an entity listing. Helium cover (R8 N19) breaches by early 2028 on allocation letters (USGS, February 2026), and the United States plant (R1 N16) follows through it. Share moves, because a competitor outside the bloc keeps delivering into Japan and India (N36, N29). Confidence moderate; the licence approval rate is unpublished. Logic stock at N17 longer than the licence queue turns the break into a cost.

**F4, a part stops, helium no longer decides.** The cold head (R5 N05) breaks by 2029 when the maker's parent allocates capacity to fusion, physics and quantum buyers and a dual-use listing closes the licence route. Here price is the mechanism: the OEM loses its allocation when a better-paying buyer bids above it, and that price is a Placeholder for Sourcing. Hospital deferral in 2027 on 2.5 percent margins (Kaufman Hall, 27 August 2026) pushes the OEM down the queue first. Both plants stop for most of 18 months and the installed base breaches service (N34). Confidence moderate on node, low on date. A Placeholder price the OEM would pay turns the break into a cost.

**Shared and particular.** R1 N16 and R2 N17 break in all four, through N19 on the helium side and N05 on the sealed side. R8 N19 belongs to F1 and F3; R5 N05 to F2 and F4; R6 N10 to F3 alone. R3 N18, R4 N03 and R7 N20 cost everywhere and break nowhere. Demand shape (C5) scales bills, not breaks.

**The encryption break (C4).** A 35-day loss of systems exceeds the line by five days, so at N16 or N17 it breaches in every future, costing one stop plus the orders bunched behind it. At the wire mill (N03) or cold-head maker (N05) it breaches only where inventory is under 35 days, a Placeholder for Sourcing. In F1 it lands worst at the gas carriers (N23) and the port terminal (N26), walking through thin cover at N19. In F2 and F4 it coincides with the cold-head break; in F3 it is more likely. Confidence moderate; the 35-day record is public, the inventories are not.

## Critical factors

The rate and price path decide how much cover sits at N19 and how deep the stock at N17 runs. The cold-head parent's selling price elsewhere decides F4. Hospital margins decide the OEM's queue position with every supplier.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the rate, producer inflation, the European replacement cohort, a sole cold-head maker whose parent earns a minority from MR. Uncertain, outcomes: the Gulf repair, the truce notice, the allocation letter. Causation: curtailment from a grid rating. Value: whether a late install is punished (N36).

## Contradictions with other lenses

On the six-to-five cold-head split, this lens holds the break in F4 and the cost in F2, because in F2 no price moves the maker. The parent's MR revenue share settles it.

## Blind spots of this lens

It assumes a price clears the cold-head allocation; a physical stop clears at no price in time. It underweights the politics of a dual-use listing.
