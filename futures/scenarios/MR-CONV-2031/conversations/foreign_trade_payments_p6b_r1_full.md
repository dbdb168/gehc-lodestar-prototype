## Analysis

Bottom line: the actions that work in all four futures need no rule to land either way: confirm the Placeholders (A10), qualify a second cold-head maker (A1), test the manual fallback (A9). The trade-specific actions, A5 and A6, are bets on F3; A5 is cheap enough to buy regardless. Ranking by futures covered, then cost of waiting: A10, A1, A9. A5 is this lens's bet.

**A1. Second cold-head maker.** Verdict: works in all four; removes the break in F2 and F4, a cost in F1 and F3. Protects N05, N16, N17; R5, R1, R2. Why now: 18 months of requalification; a start after mid-2027 misses the 2029 F4 break. Cost and time: millions, order unknown; 18 months. Start by the second quarter of 2027. Too late after the end of 2027. Act if a second maker publishes a 4 kelvin cold head of comparable rating, or the parent's annual report shows cryogenics shrinking. Pause if the sealed share stays below a tenth of builds by 2028 and no listing names cryocoolers. Look again if a producer-country dual-use notice names cryocoolers. Cost of waiting a year: both plants stopped for up to a year in F4. Panel judgement.

## Critical factors

The truce notice, any listing naming wire, cryocoolers or logic, and the unpublished licence approval rate decide whether A5 and A6 are bets or waste.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: controls are addressed by destination; 18 months of requalification. Uncertain, outcomes: the notice and listings. Uncertain, causation: queue length after a listing. Uncertain, actions: whether a non-United States logic path escapes the foreign direct product rule. Uncertain, value: whether pass-through is accepted.

## Contradictions with other lenses

The cold-head split decides whether A1 ranks first or third; this lens says break. Financial may rank A2 above A1; this lens says a contract does not survive a listing, and one refused licence would settle it.

## Blind spots of this lens

This lens overweights written rules and may miss the parent holding back cold heads for its own reasons, which A2 reads better than A1.
