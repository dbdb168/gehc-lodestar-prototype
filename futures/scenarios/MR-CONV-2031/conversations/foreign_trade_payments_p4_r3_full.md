# Foreign trade and payments, phase 4, round 3

## Analysis

Bottom line: N19 for F1, N05 for F2, N17 for F3, N05 for F4. Both F4 pages wrongly treat a listing as a stop at the United States plant.

F1. I adopt Natural disasters' helium cover at the United States plant (N19). The one trade instrument that could reach a plant here, a United States helium allocation rule, sits on U2. Financial's allocation letter at N17 is a contract term, not a rule. Panel judgement.

F2. I adopt Technology shift's cold head (N05), through a hazard only. A parent allocating output away from MR is the owner-exit cause phase 3 folded into U1 end B, so Customer and market's trigger belongs in F4. Panel judgement.

F3. I keep the China plant (N17) through United States-origin logic (N10), with Geopolitical. Security's payment freeze (N31) lands a quarter earlier if a data demand precedes a listing. Panel judgement, moderate.

F4. I adopt Macroeconomic's cold head (N05) over the Contrarian's wire (N03). A listing is a licence requirement, and an allied producer normally grants a licence to a United States plant; what stops a part is an owner's choice (P18). Panel judgement, low on the date.

Testing the four. They differ on both axes: F1 breaks on a physical margin, F2 on a hazard, F3 on a rule inside a counterparty's borders, F4 on an owner. Macroeconomic's F4 has the China plant lose a quarter on a 2027 logic listing; a quarter is past the 30-day line, so that page's first break is N17 in 2027, not N05 in 2029. Customer and market's F2 trigger is an F4 cause. Dates run in order: F3 stops in 2027, F1 breaks from the 2027 season, F4 in 2029, F2 from 2029, F4 after F3 because the sealed share must pass half.

The wrong thing. Both F4 pages stop both plants on a producer-country listing. A listing of wire or cryocoolers by an allied producer stops shipments to the China plant (N17), where end-use review bites, and delays the United States plant (N16) by the licence time. Only an owner exit or a 35-day event stops N16. AI-suggested; Quality and regulatory confirms.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the listing tools exist and were used on this sector in 2025. Uncertain, outcomes: whether the truce renews and what a notice names first. Uncertain, causation: whether a listing is enforced as a stop or a delay at an allied plant.
