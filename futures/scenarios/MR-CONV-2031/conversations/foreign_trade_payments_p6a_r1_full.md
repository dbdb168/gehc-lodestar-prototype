# Foreign trade and payments, phase 6a, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line: a trade measure is the first mover in one future (F3) and a co-mover in one more (F4). In F1 and F2 every measure this lens tracks, tariffs, customs time and payment friction at N31, stays a cost inside the 30-day line; the breaks belong to helium cover (N19) and the cold head (N05).

F1. No trade break. Tariffs on copper (N04) and semiconductors (N08, N10 to N12) raise the bill; brokers and banks (N31) add days. The first break is R8 helium cover (N19) in the winter of 2028 to 2029, a line stop of 30 to 45 days and a quarter of Western installs late. The trade contribution is the China plant's place at the back of the helium queue (R2, N17), breaching the install line in the first half of 2028 by allocation, not rule. Confidence low to moderate: cover days are a Placeholder, and a reading above 60 days removes the N19 break.

F2. No trade break. Wire (N03), cold heads (N05) and chips flow at tariff cost under a renewed truce. The first break is R5 (N05) by hazard or outage at the sole maker, 2029 to 2031, stopping both plants (R1, R2) for longer than 30 days because requalification takes 18 months. A hazard does not change a part's trade status, so no licence process offers a workaround. Confidence moderate on the node, low on the date.

F3. The trade future. The truce lapses at its notice date, first read 10 November 2026. Entity listings in 2027 name the China plant's logic supplier, and N17 cannot import United States-origin logic (N10) without a rarely granted licence. R2 breaks through R6 in the second half of 2027, within a quarter of the listing, and lasts as long as the licence queue, plausibly two quarters or more. Cost: a line stop at N17 longer than 30 days, Eastern installs late two quarters, share moving through N36. Rails (N31) split by bloc. R8 (N19) breaches second, early 2028. Confidence moderate: the listing tools reached this sector in 2025; the date hangs on the notice and an unpublished approval rate. Placeholder: the China plant's logic stock in weeks; a stock longer than the licence queue turns the break into a cost.

F4. Trade appears twice but neither measure breaks alone. The 2027 tariffs and logic listing are absorbed with stock raised after notice. R5 (N05) breaks by 2029 when the maker's parent allocates capacity elsewhere and a producer-country dual-use notice on 4 kelvin cryocoolers closes the licence route. Controls are addressed by destination, so the listing strands N17 while the allocation strands N16. Both plants stop for most of 18 months. Confidence moderate on the node, low on a listing that does not exist today. Placeholder: the price at which the OEM loses its allocation.

## Critical factors

Whether a listing names wire, niobium, cryocoolers or logic before 2029. Whether the China plant's licence approval rate stays near zero. Whether customers accept tariff pass-through (N35, N36).

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the 2025 tariff stack stays a cost; one cryocooler maker has left; controls are addressed by destination. Uncertain, outcomes: the truce notice, listings, a parent exit. Uncertain, causation: how a listing becomes a licence queue of a given length. Uncertain, value: whether a late install is punished.

## Shared and unique breaks

Shared by all four: the C4 encryption event. Shared by F1 and F3: helium cover (N19). Shared by F2 and F4: the cold head (N05). Unique to F3: the China plant (N17) through logic (N10). Unique to F4: both plants stopped by a parent exit plus a listing. Wire (N03) breaks nowhere through this lens.

## Shared encryption break (C4)

Through this lens it lands on payment rails (N31) and the port terminal (N26). A 35-day loss of a broker's or bank's systems holds parts at the border and freezes payment. In F1 and F2, 35 days at N31 is a cost if stock covers it, while 35 days at N26 holds every export magnet and breaches the install line on the Atlantic lanes. In F3 a rails outage extends the licence-queue stop. In F4 at the cold-head maker it is indistinguishable from the first break. Confidence moderate: 35 days is the longest public recovery record; stock at N31 is unknown. Every future assumes N22 intact.

## Contradictions with other lenses

Truce date, carried. Cold head at U2 end B: this lens says breaks in F4 and costs in F2, where no rule moves. Geopolitical reads a listing as a stop; this lens reads it as a licence queue whose length is the variable.

## Blind spots of this lens

Written rules over mood. A listing applied loosely, or an uncontrolled part held back by a producer, would not show on this lens's indicators.
