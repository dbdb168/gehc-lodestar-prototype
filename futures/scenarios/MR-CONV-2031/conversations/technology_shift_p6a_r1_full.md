# Technology shift, phase 6a, round 1

## Analysis

Bottom line: the helium axis decides which part is the only thermal path, the trade axis which plant a stop lands on. In all four futures one mill's wire (N03) and one maker's cold head (N05) are sole sources; no cleared product changes that by 2031 (openFDA product code LNH, read 11 October 2026). What changes is whether helium stands behind it.

F1, cost only, helium still decides. Helium cover (N19) at the United States plant (N16) breaks first, winter 2028 to 2029: a 30 to 45 day stop and a quarter of Western installs late. The China plant (N17) breaches second in the first half of 2028 as late installs on the allocation queue. Other risks cost. Confidence low to moderate: grid rating to curtailment has no record. A cover reading (Placeholder) above the container hold plus the ten-day grid rebuild removes it.

F2, cost only, helium no longer decides. Once the sealed share passes half of output in 2029, the cold head (N05) breaks first: an outage at the sole maker stops both plants (N16, N17) for longer than 30 days, since 18 months of requalification outlasts inventory. No schedule. Cover (N19) leaves the story; wire (N03) costs; technicians (N20) put installs two to three quarters late in 2029 (Labor). Confidence moderate on the node, low on the date. Placeholder: a delivery-sized test draw pulls this back to F1.

F3, a part stops, helium still decides. The China plant (N17) breaks in the second half of 2027 through logic (N10) behind an entity listing: a stop longer than 30 days, Asian installs two quarters late, share lost through N36. The United States plant cannot cover (N21). Cover (N19) breaches second by early 2028. Confidence moderate: the listing tools exist and geography fixes the plant. Placeholder: logic stock at N17 longer than the licence queue removes the break.

F4, a part stops, helium no longer decides. The cold head (N05) breaks by 2029 when the maker's parent allocates capacity away from MR and a dual-use listing closes the licence route. Both plants stop for most of 18 months and the installed base breaches service cover (N34). Wire (N03) is the Contrarian's alternative. Confidence moderate: neither event is announced. Placeholder: the price at which the OEM loses its allocation.

## Critical factors

Shared by all four: sole-source wire (N03) and cold head (N05), an intact record (N22), the encryption break. Helium side (F1, F3): cover (N19) breaches, first in F1, second in F3. Sealed side (F2, F4): the cold head breaks first, the trade axis changing the cause from hazard to allocation. Belonging to one: the China plant through logic (N17, N10) in F3; the installed base warming (N34) in F4.

The encryption break. A 35-day loss of systems at either plant (N16, N17) is a 35-day line stop in every future, since neither covers the other. At the wire mill (N03) it costs, since inventory runs months, and extends the gap in F4. At the cold-head maker (N05) it costs in F1 and F3, where helium covers the field, and is a line stop in F2 and F4, the same as the first break. At a gas carrier (N23) or the export port (N26) it is a stop in F1 and F3 only if cover is under 35 days, and weeks of delay in F2 and F4. Confidence moderate for the plants (35 days is the longest public recovery record), low for the suppliers, whose fallback is a Placeholder.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: sole-source wire and cold head, 18-month requalification, the 1.5 tesla sealed clearance of 5 February 2026, one cryocooler maker gone (P18). Uncertain, outcomes: the 3 tesla clearance date, the Gulf reopening, the truce notice. Causation: the test draw in litres; grid rating to curtailment. Value: whether a dark scanner or a late install is punished more (N36).

## Contradictions with other lenses

The six-to-five split on the cold head at U2 end B stands; this lens says break. The Contrarian's wire-first F4 strands one plant, since a listing names one destination. This lens places the sealed majority in 2029, where it is most likely wrong: hospitals buy service history, not clearances.

## Blind spots of this lens

It overrates adoption and underrates the installed base and the service network. It can miss that removing helium makes R5 a service break as much as a delivery break.
