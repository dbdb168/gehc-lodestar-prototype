# Labor, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

The US manufacturing workforce had a median age of 43.9 in 2025, about a quarter of it is 55 or older, and the cohort that turns 65 between 2025 and 2027 is the largest on record, so the oldest magnet winders and cryogenic technicians retire inside the horizon whatever else happens (N20, N16, N18, N14, N07; BLS table 18b, 2025, https://www.bls.gov/cps/cpsaat18b.htm; the OEM's own roster age is AI-suggested, Human resources to confirm).

The longshore master contract covering the Charleston export port expires on 30 September 2030, the last stoppage in October 2024 lasted three days, and the contract carries automation protections, so the port labour calendar is quiet until mid-2030 and live for the final fifteen months of the horizon (N26; ILA, 7 February 2025, https://ilaunion.org/?p=8780).

South Carolina's union membership rate was 2.8 percent in 2024 and Wisconsin's 6.4 percent in 2025, both well under the 10.0 percent national rate, which makes a bargaining stoppage at either US plant a low-base-rate event and slow attrition of untracked trades the more likely labour failure (N16, N18, N20; BLS, 28 February 2025 and 2026 tables; Panel judgement on attrition versus strike).

Regional labour markets at both plants are tight for demographic reasons that persist to 2031: Wisconsin projects a 294,000-worker gap by 2032 and lost 9,500 manufacturing jobs in 2025 mostly to retirement, and South Carolina manufacturers compete with a 4,000-worker vehicle plant for the same mechanics and electricians (N20, N18, N32; Wisconsin DWD, November 2025; WPR, 4 May 2026; Post and Courier, 2023).

The 100,000 dollar H-1B fee has been extended on paper to 21 September 2027 but is blocked by federal court orders, and Japan and Germany, home to the sole cold-head and wire sources, are both shrinking their working-age populations through the 2030s (N22, N33, N03, N05; Mintz, 23 September 2026; Statistics Bureau of Japan, 2024; KfW, 1 July 2025; supplier headcounts are AI-suggested, Sourcing to confirm).

## In one hundred words

Labour's predetermined elements are demographic and calendar facts. The manufacturing workforce is middle-aged and its oldest cohort retires before 2031, so the magnet trades at N20 lose people without any supplier failing. The Charleston longshore contract runs to 30 September 2030, putting the port bargaining cliff inside the horizon. Union density at both plant states is near the national floor, so a strike is less likely than quiet attrition. US skilled immigration is restricted and litigated, and the wire and cold-head supplier countries are shrinking their working-age populations. The OEM's roster age, supplier town dependence and automation pace remain uncertain.
