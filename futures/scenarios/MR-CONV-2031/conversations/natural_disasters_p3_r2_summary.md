# Natural disasters, phase 3, round 2, summary

## Key points

This lens accepts C1 (restriction becomes a stop) and C2 (helium stays a line risk) as the axes, and withdraws its own C7, because a grid curtailment is an event rather than a structural question and only matters on the C2 end where helium still decides shipment (N16, N18, N19; Panel judgement).

Every hazard this lens carries, the basin flood behind the US magnet plant, the winter grid emergency and a plains storm at a helium source, reaches the tolerance line through helium cover, so the reworded C2 question is whether a magnet still waits for helium before it ships in 2031 (N16, N19, N01, N23; Panel judgement, with the sealed-test consumption a Placeholder for Engineering).

The four axis combinations are all coherent, including restriction as a stop with helium no longer a risk, since the OEM's own cleared sealed option lets conversion run inside a trade wall even if a competitor's helium-light platforms cannot cross it (N17, N05, N19; FDA K253779, 5 February 2026, as carried by the technology shift lens).

C4 belongs in phase 6a as a shared break with a five-week duration, C5 is a cost modifier on every future with tender criteria as a watch item, and C7 is a modifier on the two helium-risk futures with the seasonal reliability assessment as indicator (N16, N17, N18, N03, N05, N19, N36; Panel judgement).

Correction to round 1: domestic weather at a United States helium source reaches the US plant through outage of the private domestic system, not through Gulf scarcity, which the contrarian and financial lenses show falls mainly on the China plant (N01, N16, N17, N19; USGS, February 2026, as carried).

## In one hundred words

The moderator's axes hold from the hazard side. Helium cover is the door every weather event walks through to reach the line, so whether helium still decides shipment in 2031 is the axis this lens needs, and trade restriction is independent of it in all four combinations. The grid curtailment I proposed is an event, not an axis; it modifies the two futures where helium stays a risk. The encryption event is a shared break for phase 6a. Demand shape prices every future. If helium stops being a risk, the natural hazard that remains is a Taiwan or Japanese earthquake under a foundry or cold-head address nobody has confirmed.
