# Macroeconomic, phase 3, round 1

## Analysis

Eight things on this lens can go two or more ways by 2031 and would change whether the magnetic resonance (MR) conventional magnet line delivers inside the 30-day tolerance line. The two that move the most screened risks are the hospital demand cycle and the path of the dollar and the policy rate, because each changes the volume and cost that every single source and both magnet plants carry. Helium price and a possible United States allocation rule on helium each move three. Read date 11 October 2026; figures come from the phase 2 transcripts and two direct fetches of Federal Reserve pages. No hospital capital survey, supplier accounts or producer disclosure on the Qatari helium units were found.

**U-M1. Do United States hospitals cut 2027 MR orders, delay them, or let them land on top of Europe's replacement wave?** Moves R3 (N18 hub throughput), R4 (N03 wire volume), R5 (N05 cold-head volume) and R7 (N20 hiring against a volume plan). Kind: causation, with a value element in whether tenders score reliability. Fixed: a policy rate of 4.1 percent through 2027, hospital margin of 2.5 percent in the first half of 2026, Medicaid phasing down from fiscal 2028, and over a quarter of Europe's installed base past ten years old (Federal Reserve, 16 Sep 2026; Kaufman Hall via Advisory Board, 27 Aug 2026; COCIR, 2023). Open: what a hospital does with a squeezed margin. Orders are cut, and N03 and N05 lose the volume that keeps MR a priority for their parents. Orders are deferred to 2028 and 2029 and meet the European wave, so the late build rate runs above plan and the single sources bottleneck. Orders hold flat on replacement need. Uncertain because no 2026 capital survey was retrieved; replacement need is fixed, orders are not (P17). Indicator: Kaufman Hall's monthly National Hospital Flash Report (kaufmanhall.com). Panel judgement, moderate confidence to 2027, low on orders.

**U-M2. Where do the dollar and the cost of money go, and does that move where it is cheaper to build and hold stock?** Moves R2 (N17 loading against the United States plant), R4 (N03 wire priced in euros, pounds and yen), R5 (N05 cold heads priced in yen) and R8 (N19 carrying cost of cover). Kind: outcomes. On 2 October 2026 the yen was 157.81 per dollar, the euro 1.1259 dollars, the yuan 6.7038 per dollar and the broad dollar index 121.38 (Federal Reserve H.10, 5 Oct 2026). The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) median path is 4.1 percent in 2026 and 2027, 3.9 in 2028, 3.6 in 2029 and 3.2 longer run (projections, 16 Sep 2026). Directions: the rate falls and the dollar weakens, so imported wire and cold heads cost more while stock gets cheaper to hold; producer prices at 5.4 percent a year (BLS, 10 Sep 2026) keep the rate near 4 percent and the strong dollar keeps Japanese and European inputs cheap; the yuan is revalued and the China plant's advantage narrows. Uncertain because the FOMC path has moved over a point within a year and no index forecasts currency. Indicator: the weekly H.10 and quarterly projections at federalreserve.gov. Panel judgement, moderate confidence.

**U-M3. What does a helium fill cost in 2028, and who is served first when there is not enough?** Moves R1 (N16 liquefaction and ramp), R2 (N17, in the market that lost Qatari supply) and R8 (N19 cover sized in days at a price). Kind: outcomes. It carries U-H1 into price. Fixed: Qatar's 63 of 190 million cubic metres is impaired with a stated three-to-five-year repair, Russian helium is barred from the West, and the United States reserve was sold in 2024 (USGS, Jan 2026; P3). Open: a 6 March 2026 satellite analysis saw main equipment intact and no producer disclosure confirms the helium units were hit. Directions: Qatari units restart by 2028 and the 40 to 100 percent spot premium of 2026 (WestAir, 21 May 2026, secondary) unwinds; impairment runs to 2030 and suppliers keep serving healthcare first, so the United States plant pays more but is filled while the China plant is rationed; impairment coincides with a United States producer outage and the shortage becomes physical, which no price clears in time. Indicator: the producer's force majeure notices and the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries each January. Moderate confidence on supply figures, low on timing.

**U-M4. Does the United States government steer helium to a bigger buyer than hospitals?** Moves R1, R2 and R8 on the same nodes as U-M3, by rule rather than price. Kind: a government's action, met by the OEM as an outcome. The United States is the only net-exporting producer (USGS, Jan 2026), fabs and space launch are growing users, and the 2026 Section 232 chip measures show an administration willing to steer inputs to data-centre and defence uses (EY, 15 Jan 2026). Directions: no rule; an export licence that keeps helium at home, helping N16 and starving N17; a defence or semiconductor priority rating that puts magnet plants behind fabs. Uncertain because nothing was proposed at the read date. Indicator: federalregister.gov. Panel judgement, low confidence.

**U-M5. Does a downturn or a fusion and physics boom push the wire mill or cold-head maker out of MR before demand falls?** Moves R4 (N03) and R5 (N05). Kind: outcomes, with a causation element in whether MR is a priority for the parent. Phase 2 found the one United States cryocooler maker owned by a quantum-cryogenics firm that does not list MR, and Western mills with order books filling with fusion work (P18). Directions: parents reaffirm MR; capacity moves to higher-margin physics work and MR lead times and prices rise without a formal exit; a 2027 or 2028 recession cuts expansion funding while the physics queue stays long, so an 18-month requalification starts behind other buyers. Uncertain because no balance sheet or backlog was retrieved. Indicator: parents' annual reports at sec.gov. AI-suggested on which mill is qualified; Sourcing confirms. Low confidence.

**U-M6. Do the 2027 refined copper duty and a wider chip tariff land, and is magnet wire reclassified into the copper annex?** Moves R4 (N03) and R6 (N10, if the non-data-centre exemption narrows). Kind: actions by a government. The 50 percent duty on worked copper has applied since 1 August 2025; the 15 percent refined duty for 1 January 2027 was undecided in June 2026 (White House, 30 Jul 2025; ING, Jun 2026). Directions: the refined duty lands and rises to 30 percent in 2028; it is deferred; the wire's code moves under the copper annex. Uncertain because the proclamation is unsigned. Indicator: federalregister.gov. The classification is AI-suggested; Sourcing confirms.

**U-M7. Does regional construction demand price the magnet plant's trades out of its labour market?** Moves R1 (N16) and R7 (N20). Kind: outcomes. The Carolinas grid carries contracted data-centre load from 2027 (NERC, 29 Jan 2026). Directions: data-centre building bids up welders and cryogenic technicians and attrition rises without a strike; the boom cools and the roster holds; wage inflation is absorbed. Uncertain because county wage data were not retrieved. Indicator: Bureau of Labor Statistics Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (bls.gov/cew). Panel judgement, low confidence.

**U-M8. Does memory and logic allocation to AI buyers reach lead time, not only price?** Moves R6 (N10) and watch items N11 and N12. Kind: causation. DRAM contract prices roughly tripled across 2026 under allocation reported to run into 2028 (P16). Directions: allocation ends with an AI spending break in 2027; it holds and field-programmable gate array (FPGA) lead times pass 18 months; it holds for memory only. Indicator: TrendForce quarterly bulletins. AI-suggested on whether the OEM buys on allocation; Sourcing confirms.

## Critical factors

The 2027 United States hospital capital plan (U-M1) and the FOMC path with its effect on the dollar (U-M2) set the rest. Helium (U-M3, U-M4) reaches a line stop fastest, through N19.

## Predetermined versus uncertain

Predetermined: the rate and reimbursement path to 2027, the tariffs in force, Qatari impairment at the read date, the installed base's age. Uncertain: the eight items above.

## Contradictions with other lenses

None recorded; this is round 1. The phase 2 disagreement on Qatari repair timing is carried as U-M3.

## Blind spots of this lens

I have read no supplier's accounts, so U-M5 is the weakest item. I treat U-M3 as a price question when phase 2 evidence shows allocation by supplier choice. I rank U-M4 low because no proposal exists, which is the political blind spot in my dossier.
