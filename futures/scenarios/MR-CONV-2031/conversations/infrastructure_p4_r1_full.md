### F1 Cost only, helium still decides (U1 end A, U2 end A)

What happened. 2026 to 2028: Hormuz stays near closed (four transits against 85, IMF PortWatch via straits.live, 11 Oct 2026) and the Qatari repair runs three to five years (producer statement via IOM3, 23 Mar 2026). The ISO container fleet of 500 to 700 units with a 35 to 48 day hold (P5, AI-suggested on fleet size) idles on the Gulf side and cycles hard on the Atlantic side. 2028 to 2031: the Carolinas grid is rated elevated from 2027 and the Midcontinent high from winter 2028 (NERC, 29 Jan 2026).

What is true at my nodes. Containers (N23) are the scarce asset, so cycle time sets cover at N19. The export port (N26) runs normally under the longshore contract to 2030 (P13). Liquefaction at N16 draws grid power under the elevated winter rating (P14; AI-suggested, Site operations confirms).

What breaks first. R8 helium cover (N19), through a winter curtailment or a basin flood at N16 while a container is in transit, likeliest in the winter of 2028 to 2029. Panel judgement.

What it costs. One line stop at N16 of 30 to 45 days, and one quarter of Western Hemisphere installs late.

Most confident: containers, not molecules, set the cover clock (high; P5). Least confident: whether the elevated rating becomes a multi-week curtailment (low; causation).

### F2 Cost only, helium no longer decides (U1 end A, U2 end B)

What happened. 2026 to 2028: Hormuz reopens to insured traffic, allocation letters stop, a 3 tesla sealed magnet is cleared. 2028 to 2031: sealed builds draw a cylinder at test, not a delivery (Placeholder; Engineering sets the litres), and containers return to a buyers' market.

What is true at my nodes. N23 and N19 leave the line clock. Oversize lanes N24 and N25 are unchanged; permit delays stay days. The export port (N26) remains the one Atlantic gate, and the only 30-day closure class is a blocked channel (P13).

What breaks first. R1 US magnet plant (N16), through a basin flood that cuts grid, road and trailer access together (P14), in any hurricane season. No lane breaks on a schedule; this is the quiet future. Panel judgement.

What it costs. A flood breaches 30 days only if road access stays cut longer than the ten-day Helene grid record (P14).

Most confident: the export port is not a 30-day risk (high; P13). Least confident: whether the sealed test draw is a cylinder (low; causation Placeholder).

### F3 A part stops, helium still decides (U1 end B, U2 end A)

What happened. 2026 to 2028: the truce lapses at the first notice date (10 November 2026 or 10 January 2027, unresolved) and an entity listing cuts cold heads and logic into the China plant (N17); Hormuz stays near closed. 2028 to 2031: a United States helium allocation rule makes cover at the United States plant a licensed quantity.

What is true at my nodes. The Asian lanes (N29) carry fewer magnets because N17 cannot build them. Cover at N19 is a licensed number, not a stock.

What breaks first. R2 China plant (N17), through cold heads (N05) under the listing, inside the 18-month replacement window, by mid-2028. The second break is R8 cover (N19) once allocation starts, around 2029. Panel judgement.

What it costs. Eastern Hemisphere deliveries stop past 30 days with no second plant (N29).

Most confident: a border stop on cold heads strands N17 before any lane fails (high; node map, AI-suggested). Least confident: the form and timing of an allocation rule (low; outcomes).

### F4 A part stops, helium no longer decides (U1 end B, U2 end B)

What happened. 2026 to 2028: sealed conversion finishes behind a trade wall and a 3 tesla sealed clearance lands. 2028 to 2031: a producer country lists cold heads, or the cold-head plant's parent leaves MR (P18).

What is true at my nodes. The cold head carries every magnet's thermal load, so air freight (N30) is the only lane that matters. N23 and N19 are out of the line clock.

What breaks first. R5 cold heads (N05), through the listing or the owner exit, by 2029. The lane carrying them has four carriers and is never the limiting asset. Panel judgement, moderate (six to five split in phase 3).

What it costs. A line stop at both plants for the listing's duration less cold-head stock on hand (Placeholder; Sourcing). Every install late.

Most confident: no lane on my map limits this future; a part does (high). Least confident: whether cold-head stock covers 30 days (low; Placeholder).
