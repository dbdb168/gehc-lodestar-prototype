# Financial, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

The United States helium system is privately owned since June 2024, there is no government stockpile, and Russian helium is barred from the United States and the European Union, so the only buffers left are contracts and the OEM's own cover (N01, N19, N34; USGS Feb 2026, Messer 27 Jun 2024).

Qatar produced about a third of world helium in 2025, all of it ships through Hormuz, and the March 2026 strikes on Ras Laffan showed the route can close while production restarted only around June 2026 (N01, N23, N27; USGS Feb 2026, Euronews 25 Mar 2026, QatarEnergy Jun 2026).

Niobium is 93% Brazilian from a family-controlled private producer with Chinese and Japanese-Korean minorities, and the Western wire mills belong to listed parents whose superconductor backlogs are filling with fusion and physics orders (N02, N03; USGS Feb 2026, Bruker 10-K FY2025, AI-suggested for the OEM's qualified mill).

The one United States cryocooler maker was bought in March 2023 by a quantum-cryogenics company that does not list MR among its markets, so the cold-head second source has already changed hands (N05, N06; Bluefors 28 Mar 2023).

Working capital for cover is priced off a policy rate of 3.75 to 4.00% as of September 2026, and United States hospital demand carries a legislated Medicaid phase-down from fiscal 2028 to 2032 (N19, N35; Federal Reserve 16 Sep 2026, HANYS Oct 2025, Panel judgement on timing).

## In one hundred words

By 2031 the financial facts are mostly set: helium has no public owner and a third of it sits behind one strait, niobium is a private Brazilian family asset, the wire mills answer to listed parents with non-MR order books, and the spare cryocooler maker already belongs to a quantum-research buyer. Cover is financed near 4% and United States hospital capital weakens on a statutory schedule from 2028. Still open: whether the cold-head maker or a wire mill is sold or exits MR, how tariffs on devices and refined copper land, and whether any insurer writes non-damage supply cover.
