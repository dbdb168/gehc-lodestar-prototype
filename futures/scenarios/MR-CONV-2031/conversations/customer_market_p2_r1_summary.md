# Customer and market, phase 2, round 1, summary

## Key points

European public tenders for medical devices at or above 5 million euros have excluded Chinese-origin systems since 30 June 2025 with no expiry on the regulation's face, so the China plant is a regional plant, not a backup for Europe (N17, N28, N36; EUR-Lex, 20 June 2025, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2025/1197/oj/eng).

The United States opened a Section 232 investigation on 2 September 2025 that names MRI machines in scope, and as of 11 October 2026 no proclamation on medical devices has followed, so tariff status is now a procurement input with an open outcome (N18, N31, N35; Federal Register, 26 Sept 2025, https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/26/2025-18729/notice-of-request-for-public-comments-on-section-232-national-security-investigation-of-imports-of).

More than one fourth of Europe's MRI installed base was already over 10 years old in 2023, and every magnet installed from 2016 to 2021 will pass that line by 2031, which fixes replacement need but not orders (N35, N26, N28; COCIR, 2023 edition, https://www.cocir.org/wp-content/uploads/23049_COC_Medical-Imaging_Age-Profile-Density-combined.pdf).

The English NHS has stated it will not buy from suppliers outside its net zero commitment before the end of this decade, which brings helium and service carbon into tender scoring (N36, N34, N33; NHS England, Oct 2020, https://www.england.nhs.uk/greenernhs/wp-content/uploads/sites/51/2020/10/delivering-a-net-zero-national-health-service.pdf).

No public tender framework retrieved this round scores delivery reliability explicitly, so the cost of a late delivery is paid through re-tender behaviour rather than a published formula (N36; Panel judgement).

## In one hundred words

By 2031 the buyers of MR scanners will be older populations served by older fleets, scored through rules that already weigh origin, carbon and tariff status. The European exclusion of Chinese-origin devices above 5 million euros is in force and open-ended; the US Section 232 file names MRI but has not concluded; Medicare 2026 rates carry a productivity cut; China funds renewal to 2027; the NHS ties purchasing to net zero by 2030. Delivery reliability is nowhere scored on paper, which makes its cost real but invisible. Open questions are tariff outcomes, Chinese counter-measures, helium-free requirements and fleet replacement.
