# Infrastructure, phase 6b, round 1, summary

## Key points

Confirming the Placeholders (A10) works in all four futures and costs nothing, and until cover days at N19 and backup power at N16 are stated every other action is sized to a guess (N19, N16, N21; Placeholder).

Testing a 30-day manual fallback at both plants, both sole suppliers and the gas carriers (A9) works in all four futures because the shared encryption break has no external clock, and this lens says it bites hardest at the carriers where the cover runs out (N16, N17, N23, N26; Panel judgement).

Helium cover above the container hold plus the grid rebuild (A3) and backup power for the liquefier (A4) work in F1 and F3, the futures the read date leans toward, and must exist before winter 2028 (N19, N16; Placeholder until Logistics and Site operations confirm).

Ramp and test capacity to cover Asia (A8) works in F3 and F4 but raises helium draw at the United States plant, so the 2027 capital plan must size A8 and A3 together (N21, N29, N16; AI-suggested, Site operations confirms).

Two actions are missing: a second cryogenic carrier with an inland transfer point for F1 and F3, and a pre-permitted oversize route to a second export port, which covers delay in all four futures (N23, N25, N26; Panel judgement).

## In one hundred words

Through the infrastructure lens the first move costs a phone call: confirm the cover days, the backup power and the test draw, because everything else is sized from them. Second is a tested manual fallback, which protects every future and bites hardest at the carriers. Third is helium cover and liquefier backup power before winter 2028, which pay in the two futures the read date leans toward. The cold-head actions pay only once helium leaves the story. Capacity to cover Asia from the United States raises helium draw, so it and the cover must be sized together in 2027.
