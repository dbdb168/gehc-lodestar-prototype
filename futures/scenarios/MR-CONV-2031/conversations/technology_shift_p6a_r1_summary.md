# Technology shift, phase 6a, round 1, summary

## Key points

In every future the magnet is wound from one mill's wire and kept cold by one maker's cold head, and no cleared product changes that by 2031, so the helium axis only decides whether a helium reserve stands behind the cold head. N03, N05 (openFDA product code LNH, read 11 October 2026)

On the helium side, cover at the United States plant breaks first in F1 in the winter of 2028 to 2029 and second in F3 by early 2028, and a cover reading above the container hold plus the grid rebuild removes the F1 break. N19, N16, N17 (Panel judgement, Placeholder on cover days)

On the sealed side, the cold head breaks first in both F2 and F4, stopping both plants for longer than 30 days, with the trade axis changing the cause from hazard to allocation and a delivery-sized test draw pulling F2 back to F1. N05, N16, N17, N34 (Panel judgement, moderate)

The China plant through logic is the one break that belongs to a single future, F3, in the second half of 2027, and logic stock longer than the licence queue removes it. N17, N10 (AI-suggested, Sourcing confirms)

The 35-day encryption break is a line stop at either plant in all four futures, a cost at the sole suppliers where helium covers the field and a stop at the cold-head maker where it does not. N16, N17, N03, N05, N23, N26 (Panel judgement, moderate for plants, low for suppliers)

## In one hundred words

Two parts are fixed across all four futures: the wire and the cold head stay sole source to 2031. Helium decides only whether a reserve stands behind the cold head. Where it does, cover at the United States plant breaks (F1 first, F3 second); where it does not, the cold head stops both plants (F2 by hazard, F4 by allocation). The China plant through logic is F3's own break. The encryption break stops either plant everywhere and the cold-head maker on the sealed side. Placeholders that remove breaks: cover days, the test draw, logic stock at the China plant.
