# Macroeconomic, phase 3, round 2, summary

## Key points

This lens accepts C1 (restriction becomes a stop) and C2 (helium stays a line risk) as the axes, on condition that C1 is worded to cover parts and finished scanners only, because a United States export rule on helium is a restriction that belongs to C2 and would otherwise make the axes overlap (N17, N03, N05, N19; Panel judgement).

All four corners of the grid can occur, since an export listing can strand the China plant's parts whether or not sealed magnets have retired the helium cover, and Qatari impairment with allocation can keep helium a risk under fully open trade (N17, N16, N19, N34; Panel judgement, moderate confidence).

Hospital demand, my round 1 leader, moves what every one of the eight breaks costs and not whether any of them breaks, except through the volume it takes from the wire mill and cold-head maker, so it is a modifier on every future with its volume element carried into C6 as a watch item (N35, N36, N03, N05; Panel judgement, correcting round 1 after the Customer and market lens).

An encryption event is a shared break for phase 6a because it is uncorrelated with rates, currency or trade and would sit the same way in every corner, and grid curtailment is a watch item that becomes a modifier on the two helium-stays futures because it bites through the cover (N16, N18, N19; NERC 29 Jan 2026; Panel judgement).

A Gulf helium shortage reaches the China plant before the United States cover, since Qatar's share of United States imports fell to 28 percent while Canada's rose to 47 percent, which corrects my round 1 treatment of the two plants as symmetric (N17, N19; USGS Feb 2026).

## In one hundred words

The moderator's axes stand. Restriction as a stop, narrowed to parts and scanners, and helium as a line risk are independent and between them move seven of eight risks. My own round 1 leaders fall away: demand moves the bill, not the break, and the dollar and rate path prices every corner without opening one. Three corrections: demand is consequence only, Gulf helium hits the China plant first, and helium is a physical litres-at-test question before it is a price. The encryption event is a shared break in every future; grid curtailment is a watch item that modifies the helium-stays futures.
